# Hidden gems: low-frequency ideas with concrete evidence

This is a 15-idea review of clusters that are either backed by one video or include an idea mentioned in passing. The frequency rank and source count come from `ideas_scored.csv` / `ideas_scored.json`; quotes below are fetched directly from `learnings.jsonl` and must be marked verified, first-hand and non-hypothetical. Podcast claims are not independently audited.

**UK marketing screen.** I treated Ricardo's stated rule as binding: no unsolicited marketing SMS, WhatsApp or social DMs to individuals or sole traders; use cold email only for limited companies. ICO guidance also treats email, text and social direct messages as electronic mail and distinguishes individual from corporate subscribers ([ICO guidance on electronic mail marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/electronic-and-telephone-marketing/electronic-mail-marketing/)). Personal-data processing needs a valid lawful basis and transparent privacy information ([ICO guide to lawful basis](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/)).

**How to read this list.** Automation figures are the catalogue's weighted scores, not a promise of unattended operation. Some entries are conditional watchlist items: the evidence is interesting but their source channel, traction, UK fit or privacy controls do not yet pass the bar for a build. The final five are the ones I would investigate first.

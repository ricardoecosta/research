3. Look for a specific problem where a customer might pay for proof that content is real or belongs to them.

- *automation_ai*: A legitimate business can use AI for content, workflow automation, and services without relying on criminal shortcuts.
- *legal_risk*: Treat verification as a process with multiple paths; a convincing voice or video is not proof by itself.
- *legal_risk*: Do not assume that a technical exploit will stay hidden; the host says exploits are reported, patched, and cataloged.
- *validation_testing*: Start with a specific problem in your own work or business and record it before trying to build a solution.
- *legal_risk*: Avoid misleading claims about AI capabilities; the host describes AI washing as an enforcement target.
- *mindset_execution*: Build a business around a real problem and durable customer value instead of chasing shortcuts.

## Warnings and mistakes

- The host warns that criminal use of AI can lead to arrest, prison, and asset loss.
- Do not use AI to create scams, impersonations, non-consensual imagery, or deceptive investment claims.
- The host says attempts to exploit AI guardrails are reported and patched, so evasion should not be treated as a sustainable business tactic.
- The host says victims may respond to unsolicited messages and be shown fabricated investment returns on polished fake platforms.
- The host warns that a video call alone may not prove that the people on it are real.
- The host promotes his own AI Escape Plan newsletter, creating a promotional conflict.

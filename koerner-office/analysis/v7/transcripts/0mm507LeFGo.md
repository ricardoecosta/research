## Tactics and playbook steps

**Automate savings on payday** (finance)
1. Set an automatic transfer from each paycheck into savings.
2. Use about 15% of take-home pay (e.g. $540 of $3,600).
3. Hold the money in a high-yield savings account.
4. Aim to never see the money so no discipline is needed.
> "set up automatic transfers so that every paycheck would go into your savings" — Casper Capital (~2:09)

**Staged emergency fund** (finance)
1. Start with a $1,000 mini buffer.
2. Build to one month of essential expenses.
3. Then 3 months if single with few responsibilities, or 6 months if income is unstable or you have kids.
4. Pay down credit card debt first.
> "Start with $1,000 as a mini buffer, then build to one month of essential expenses." — Casper Capital (~1:33)

**Treat side hustle income as non-existent** (mindset)
1. Track extra side hustle income.
2. Send most or all of it to savings.
3. Live as if it does not exist.
> "By treating side hustle income as money that doesn't exist" — Casper Capital (~4:21)

## Lessons

- *mindset_execution*: Break a large savings goal into daily or weekly amounts to make it feel achievable.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Automating transfers removes reliance on willpower and prevents spending the money first.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Hold an emergency fund before investing so a shock does not force selling investments or taking debt.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Cutting expenses alone is slow; raising income via side hustles speeds up reaching a savings target.
- *mindset_execution*: Start side hustles small and build momentum; early income may be zero.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Compounding rewards starting early; steady monthly contributions matter more than lump sums.
- *other*: Treat promoted financial products (affiliate links) with caution and compare rates yourself; UK equivalents are cash ISAs and easy-access savings accounts.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Pay down credit card debt before saving or investing, since avoiding interest is a guaranteed return.
- Build an emergency fund before taking investing risk.
- Relying on discipline to save is where most people fail; automate instead.

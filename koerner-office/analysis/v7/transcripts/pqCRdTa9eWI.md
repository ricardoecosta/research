## Tactics and playbook steps

**Build a local dog-care reputation** (acquisition)
1. Create a profile on Rover, Wag, or a community app, using photos and short videos.
2. Discount the first few walks to earn early reviews.
3. Use Google Calendar to organise visits and send a short text or photo after each walk.
4. Invest in leashes, treats, and safety gear; ask satisfied clients for referrals.
5. Create an Instagram account or website with pet photos and testimonials.
6. Add overnight sitting or small dog group hikes as capacity grows, and hire help when needed.
7. Track income and expenses; the host suggests setting up an LLC once the business is steady.

**Practise for entry-level remote sales** (hiring)
1. Study sales psychology and communication using online resources and books.
2. Use a CRM such as GoHighLevel, HubSpot, or Salesforce to track customers.
3. Apply for sales development representative or appointment setter roles.
4. Practise daily and record yourself answering common questions to improve under pressure.
5. Progress toward higher-ticket sales, sales management, or an online sales agency.

**Train through an electrical apprenticeship** (hiring)
1. Apply for an apprenticeship through IBEW, NCCER, or a local trade school.
2. Learn safety, wiring, blueprints, and installation basics from mentors.
3. After the apprenticeship, obtain the relevant licence and consider a specialism such as solar, EV charging, or smart home systems.
4. Join trade communities or unions and build relationships for jobs and referrals.
5. After gaining experience, consider project management or starting a contracting business.

**Create a digital marketing portfolio** (acquisition)
1. Take free certifications from Google, HubSpot Academy, or Meta Blueprint.
2. Try short-form video, social posts, SEO, and simple websites to find a suitable specialism.
3. Offer free or low-cost help to local businesses or creators.
4. Share before-and-after examples, growth numbers, and case studies on LinkedIn, Instagram, or Upwork.
5. Choose a specialism such as personal branding, content creation, or social media management.

## Summary

Chris Koerner chats with Sam (an agency operator, Service Scalers) about AI-generated content. Topics: licensing your cloned voice on ElevenLabs for passive income, AI-generated 'boomer bait' animal videos on Facebook monetised by impressions, and Sam's stack for AI-cloned spokesperson video ads (ChatGPT, Photoshop, face swap, ElevenLabs voice-to-voice, HeyGen Avatar 4) that he says already book calls at about $10. ## Tactics and playbook steps

**High-fidelity AI spokesperson ad pipeline** (product)
1. Generate base image in ChatGPT
2. Expand the frame with Photoshop generative expand to leave room for text
3. Face-swap the target person onto the image
4. Record the script yourself with desired pauses, then convert via ElevenLabs voice-to-voice (not text-to-speech)
5. Feed image and audio into HeyGen Avatar 4
6. Add subtitles to draw attention away from mouth tells
7. Use podcast-style setting since studio-quality audio fits
> "everybody's just being lazy with 11 Labs because they are doing text to speech." — Sam (~11:39)

**Test many ad hooks/headlines with AI video** (acquisition)
1. Produce about 15 variations of hooks and headlines
2. Run them as ads and track booked-call cost
3. Keep the winners and measure show rate

**Billboard creative and placement testing** (acquisition)
1. Run Facebook ads with 30 variations of billboard creative to a local non-follower audience for two days
2. Pick the winning headline ('get rich')
3. Book the billboard for three months
4. Track per-episode downloads in the area in a spreadsheet
5. Move to a better-visibility spot if there's no lift

**Publish first in other languages to protect content** (legal)
1. Dub your content into target languages with ElevenLabs
2. Post it first so you can file copyright claims against copycats
> "if you post it first in that language, then you can." — Chris Koerner (~3:37)

**Use less polished voices in ads** (product)
1. Pick non-studio, everyday-sounding voices instead of deep announcer voices
> "I don't want Morgan Freeman reading my ads." — Chris Koerner (~2:20)

## Lessons

- *automation_ai*: Judge AI content tools by hit rate: what share of generated outputs is good enough to trust.
- *validation_testing*: Good enough to convert beats perfect: judge AI creative by ad performance, not nitpicks.
- *customer_acquisition*: Unconventional channels (billboards) can beat Facebook ads on cost per download, but placement can swing results a lot.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Business is a maths equation: understand people and track the right metrics (CAC, LTV, payback).
- *finance_unit_economics*: Ads that look like they fail often fail because of cash conversion cycle, not the channel.
- *mindset_execution*: Do interesting public things: secondary benefits (community, audience) can justify a project that isn't itself very profitable.
- *customer_acquisition*: Repetition matters in advertising, but frequent repeat viewers waste spend; consider frequency and unique reach.
- *validation_testing*: Test many creative variants (30 billboard designs) cheaply with ads before committing to expensive media.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Out-of-the-box AI video tools have low hit rates and charge a premium; Sam went direct to underlying models.
- Text-to-speech in ElevenLabs creates unnatural inflection; studio-quality audio in a selfie-style video is a tell.
- Anyone can clone your voice from your published podcasts anyway.
- Facebook ads for podcasts fail mainly from cash conversion cycle and payback issues, not the channel itself.

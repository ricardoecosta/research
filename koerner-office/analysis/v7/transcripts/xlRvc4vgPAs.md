## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Test opportunities by asking the specific owner, manager, or customer what they currently do and what it costs before buying equipment or signing a lease.
- *niche_selection*: Look for underused assets and connect people who need access with people who control the asset.
- *pricing*: Do not assume the first easement offer is the market rate; check regional evidence and negotiate.
- *finance_unit_economics*: A slow-maturing asset can discourage competitors, but it also delays returns and requires patience.
- *offer_design*: Build the offer around a concrete unmet need, such as same-day equipment or local storage with retrieval.
- *offer_design*: Partnership terms can lower a host's barrier to saying yes; the pool-table pitch offers a split with no upfront cost to the bar.
- *legal_risk*: Before signing a long lease, inspect the escalation clause and negotiate annual increases where appropriate.
- *mindset_execution*: An inexpensive first step can be information gathering rather than buying or building an asset.

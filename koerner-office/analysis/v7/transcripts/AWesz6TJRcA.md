## Tactics and playbook steps

**Free first client as test case and referral engine** (acquisition)
1. Pick a well-connected local business owner in your circle, ideally in a shared building with many other businesses
2. Build the website and voice agent for free and say upfront it is a test
3. Ask what they would pay to gauge pricing
4. Let them demo it to neighbours and refer you
> "you're my lead magnet. Not only do you have 20 other businesses in here" — Danny (~9:00)

**Build before the meeting** (sales)
1. Research prospect's current website with ChatGPT for flaws
2. Create a Replit mockup before the first meeting and show it via link
3. Hold a roughly one-hour discovery conversation about goals and year plans
4. Build something to show and reconnect in a week or two to close
> "just showing your potential and they're like man this is amazing it looks good." — Danny (~11:45)

**Live voice agent demo while getting a haircut** (sales)
1. Set up the agent on your phone with the prospect's business name and website
2. Call the shop's number while they cut your hair and have them talk to it
3. Show the transcript afterwards
4. Close the sale with the demo
> "you open up high level on your phone and you go to the settings for the voice agent and you change out the name of the business to his business name" — Chris Koerner (~21:12)

**Ring-then-agent call routing** (operations)
1. Connect the owner's work number to Go High Level
2. Let it ring a few times so the owner can answer
3. If unanswered, route to the voice agent instead of voicemail
4. Agent texts the booking link to the caller

**Add KPI dashboard and fear metrics** (sales)
1. Build a dashboard in Replit with metrics relevant to the business
2. Show potential lost revenue from unbooked days
3. Use ChatGPT to suggest actions like targeted days or texts

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Start with a free, excellent build for someone in your sphere; if it is magical you get referrals or paid work, if not you learn.
- *offer_design*: Showing finished work before the sale makes value visible and triggers reciprocity and confirmation bias, while reducing post-sale work.
- *niche_selection*: Solve current, concrete problems for existing businesses instead of inventing new products.
- *niche_selection*: AI makes agency services viable for solo businesses that could never afford agencies, trading ticket size for numbers.
- *hiring_outsourcing*: Pair a sales-focused founder with a technical partner to cover the last mile of builds.
- *mindset_execution*: Do not wait to learn everything before starting; get hands dirty.
- *customer_acquisition*: A long discovery conversation about the owner's goals reveals problems they cannot name themselves.
- *scaling*: Opportunity windows (Yelp, PPC, voice agents) are short but later entrants still succeed.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Do not charge cash-strapped startups all their money; give advice instead.
- Opportunity windows close as competition and easier tools arrive, so move quickly.
- Medical practices need human handoff rather than a pure AI agent.
- Replit alone can struggle to finish builds, so a coding partner helps.

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Every technology shift creates new problems; the businesses solving them can be bigger opportunities than using the technology directly.
- *legal_risk*: Rising AI fraud increases scrutiny on all AI businesses: expect suspicious customers, restrictive platforms and aggressive regulators.
- *legal_risk*: Use multi-factor verification internally; standalone identity checks are no longer reliable.
- *offer_design*: Proving authenticity (human origin, identity) is becoming valuable as seeing and hearing are no longer proof.
- *mindset_execution*: Sustainable money comes from legitimate businesses built with AI, not shortcuts.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Using AI for fraud, impersonation or deceptive schemes leads to prosecution; enforcement in 2026 is aggressive.
- AI washing (overstating your product's AI capabilities to investors) is being prosecuted by the SEC, DOJ and FTC.
- Operating in grey areas now paints a target on your back as platforms and regulators tighten.
- Jailbreaks and exploits get patched and catalogued; you won't outsmart the systems.
- Pig-butchering scams target people who reply to unsolicited messages on dating apps or LinkedIn; customers will be more suspicious of unsolicited outreach.
- Creators' published audio is training data for voice cloning; video calls are no longer proof of identity.

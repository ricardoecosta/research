## Tactics and playbook steps

**Search-gap product selection** (product)
1. Use social media and a keyword tool such as Ahrefs to find rising search trends.
2. Filter for high monthly volume and low keyword difficulty (0-3 out of 100).
3. Google the term and check for few or no ads and no dedicated brand; Amazon ranking first signals no real brand.
4. Build a branded site around that specific term.
> "I use social media and keyword research tools to find products that have high search volume and low competition" — Tim Richard (~0:20)

**Start with paid ads while SEO builds** (acquisition)
1. Check that no advertisers bid on the term, so cost per click should be cheap.
2. Run Google ads immediately.
3. Profit from ad sales until the site ranks organically.
> "Get in, start doing ads right off the bat until your website can rank, and start making some profit off those ads." — Tim Richard (~11:58)

**Subscription for consumable products** (pricing)
1. For consumables like shampoo, offer a recurring plan.
2. Treat each subscriber as ongoing revenue.

**Single hero product plus buyer research (Nugget Couch model)** (product)
1. Pick one simple product that meets the searched need.
2. Research why people search the term and what they want.
3. Build that product and brand the site around it.
> "I would look into why exactly people are looking for floor couch and floor sofa." — Tim Richard (~13:42)

**Content site plus products** (acquisition)
1. Publish recipes and guides around the trend term.
2. Place your own products on the same site.

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Search volume combined with low keyword difficulty and few dedicated brands is a cheap way to screen product niches before investing.
- *niche_selection*: When Amazon or generic retailers rank first, Google lacks a dedicated brand for the term, which signals an opening for a focused site.
- *customer_acquisition*: Where no advertisers bid, paid ads can bridge the gap while organic ranking builds.
- *pricing*: Consumable products suit subscriptions, giving recurring revenue.
- *offer_design*: A single hero product aimed at an unmet search need (the Nugget Couch approach) beats a broad catalogue.
- *validation_testing*: Keyword demand shows interest, not purchase intent or margin; it must still be validated with real sales tests, and search data is region-specific.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Higher-margin categories like jewelry give more room for ad spend.

## Warnings and mistakes

- The picks are the host's opinion only; starting one does not guarantee money, so do your own research.
- Any business still takes a lot of work and effort.

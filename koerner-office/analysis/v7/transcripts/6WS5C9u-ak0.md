## Tactics and playbook steps

**Beginner straw-bucket oyster grow** (product)
1. Buy a grow kit (spawn), straw, a pot and a 5-gallon bucket
2. Boil straw 20-30 minutes, let it soak ~10 hours, drain
3. Layer straw with broken-up spawn to overwhelm contaminants
4. Lid on, drill a few holes; mushrooms push out in ~10 days

**Scale with outsourced spawn and DIY automation** (operations)
1. Buy spawn from a professional lab to avoid contamination
2. Build a pasteuriser from a barrel and propane burner
3. Build a hopper bagger to mix hardwood, soy hulls and water per species
4. Work in front of a HEPA flow hood and heat-seal bags

**Win restaurant accounts** (sales)
1. Bring fresh product in person with a positive attitude
2. Visit at quiet times, not during prep or service
3. Expect to return 2-3 times before getting a yes
4. Deliver consistently every week; first account has lasted five years

**Barter for grow space** (finance)
1. Ask a neighbour farm owner for space
2. Trade animal care for free space, power, water and AC

**Harvest timing for premium presentation** (operations)
1. Pick oysters when caps start to tilt up
2. Twist and pull at the base, trim substrate
3. Sell within a day or two while caps are folded and vibrant

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Start tiny (a 20 USD kit) and grow equipment only as skill improves.
- *operations*: Outsource the most failure-prone step (spawn) to protect output.
- *customer_acquisition*: Relationships and showing up repeatedly win restaurant accounts.
- *customer_acquisition*: Farmers markets double as a channel to meet chefs and wholesale buyers.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Barter labour for expensive overhead like space.
- *mindset_execution*: Free online learning (YouTube) is enough to build a production business.
- *niche_selection*: Pick a beginner product that is resilient and premium-priced (oyster mushrooms).
- *validation_testing*: Early validation from a first paying customer matters more than the amount.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Sloppy sterile technique led to mould wiping out a crop, costing money and reputation with chefs.
- Do not overspend on equipment before you learn to grow.
- Limit time in the grow room because of spores.
- Retail markets pay more but tie up your time; wholesale frees time at lower prices.
- Revenue is heavily seasonal; 10k/month is only at peak.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Choosing a gold IRA provider** (finance)
1. Get setup, annual admin and storage fees in writing
2. Ask for the buy/sell spread in writing
3. Confirm eligible coins/bars, shipping, insurance and timelines
4. Fund via direct transfer rollover to avoid withholding
5. Choose commingled (cheaper) or segregated storage

## Lessons

- *finance_unit_economics*: Gold is a hedge and diversifier, not a growth engine; broad indexes have outperformed it long term.
- *legal_risk*: Always get fees and spreads in writing before buying through a dealer.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Younger investors chasing growth are usually better in index funds.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Direct transfers are the safest way to roll over retirement money.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Dealers have been fined for hidden markups on coins sold to retirees.
- Avoid collectibles and proof coins, which cost more but don't resell for more.
- Gold IRA fees are much higher than index-fund IRAs; gold pays no income and can be illiquid.
- IRA gold cannot be stored at home.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Validate product demand with Google Trends then refine keywords** (product)
1. Search the problem phrase (porch pirate) in Google Trends over 5 and 22 years
2. Search the product phrase (porch pirate box) to see if recent interest is rising
3. Use the category names the sourcing AI returns (parcel box, parcel locker) and check them in Trends too
> "I'm going to go over here to Google Trends, search parcel boxes, and that too is up and to the right." — Chris Koerner (~4:28)

**Price-check supplier results against Amazon** (pricing)
1. Search the product category on Amazon and note sponsored versus organic listings
2. Check whether residential versions are advertised; gaps signal opportunity
3. Compare supplier price to top Amazon price for gross margin
4. Use a third-party Amazon photo search app with the supplier image URLs to find exact replicas
5. Check units sold last month on top listings
> "copy the URLs of all these pictures that Axio spits out and search for them through this third party app" — Chris Koerner (~7:32)

**Use AI to analyse why one identical listing outsells another** (sales)
1. Screenshot two similar listings side by side
2. Upload to an AI and ask why one sold more
3. Remove sales numbers and ask which sold more to avoid bias
> "screenshot each of these side by side, upload them to AI and say, "Why did the one on the right sell more than the one on the left?"" — Chris Koerner (~7:59)

**Sample before committing to a minimum order** (operations)
1. Find a vendor who sends a free or about $100 sample
2. Shoot photos and videos with the sample
3. Test demand with content before ordering the 100-unit MOQ

**Generate a tech pack with AI for a custom variant** (product)
1. Choose a base design from the sourcing results
2. Prompt the AI to build a full tech pack with new finish and materials
3. Send the tech pack to manufacturers to quote a custom product

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Check demand with search-trend data before investing in a product idea rather than relying on gut feel.
- *niche_selection*: Ride a rising trend with a narrower product phrase; interest in the specific product term can be exploding even when the broad problem is flat.
- *offer_design*: Sourcing an existing product instead of designing and manufacturing one removes the hardest steps for a first physical product.
- *niche_selection*: Sponsored listings of commercial versions with no ads for residential versions hint at an underserved niche, and advertisers imply the category is profitable.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Gross margin looks big but net after shipping, fees and ads is much smaller (20-30% claimed), so model net margin before ordering.
- *customer_acquisition*: Emotional viral content angles (anger, humour, curiosity) can be a cheap acquisition route for physical products.
- *automation_ai*: AI agents can automate supplier discovery, inquiries and spec documents, shrinking the gap between idea and selling a product.
- *operations*: Time-zone gaps with Asian suppliers mean auto-replies and overnight responses speed up supplier communication.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Don't spend on a 100-unit minimum order before demand is proven; get a sample and test with content first.
- Anecdotal evidence of demand may not be real evidence, so check data such as Google Trends.

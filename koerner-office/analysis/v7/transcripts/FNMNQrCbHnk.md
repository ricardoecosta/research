## Tactics and playbook steps

**Free-sample AI UGC outreach** (acquisition)
1. Browse the Facebook Ads Library for companies running many ads
2. Prefer smaller companies so you can reach the CEO
3. Estimate app ad budgets from Sensor Tower revenue x 0.6-0.7
4. Recreate one of their existing UGC ads with Arcads
5. Send it free to the CEO on LinkedIn
6. Repeat for 100 companies; sell 20-ad packages or $3-5k/month retainers
> "I will tell them, okay, this UC ad that you probably paid $500 for and that took you three weeks to to get, I will do it for you" — Romain Torres (~36:36)

**Automated ad factory** (operations)
1. Collect high-performing existing ads (longest-running first) in a Google Sheet
2. Have ChatGPT read the transcripts and write new scripts for your offer
3. Call the Arcads API through Zapier/Make/n8n/Gumloop
4. Generate 100-500 ads a day and test for winners
> "connect to our API through an automation tool like Zapier, Make.com, NA10, Gumloop and create these ads automatically" — Romain Torres (~38:50)

**Visual hooks in AI ads** (product)
1. Add a gesture in the first second (pointing at camera)
2. Put the actor in an odd costume or setting (cosmonaut, Indiana Jones, skateboard)
3. A/B test hooks
4. Use emotion and accent tags so the voice is not robotic
> "that's what we call a visual hook" — Romain Torres (~25:38)

**Niche finding for AI influencers** (acquisition)
1. Use TikTok Creator Search Insights to find searches with few videos
2. Pick a trending niche
3. Find outlier videos (10x others) on accounts, log them in a Google Sheet and replicate
4. Post consistently and iterate with emotional scripts
> "It will literally tell you which pieces of content are lacking from the platform" — Romain Torres (~34:19)

**Match AI ads to less AI-sensitive audiences** (acquisition)
1. Prefer audiences less likely to notice AI (older, non-native English)
2. Or sell AI products where AI ads are 'dogfooding'
3. Avoid sophisticated categories where AI looks low effort

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Pick audiences and categories where AI-generated creative is accepted or irrelevant.
- *customer_acquisition*: Relevance beats realism: AI is fine if it solves the viewer's problem, like any ad spokesperson.
- *pricing*: Sell to companies that already spend budget on the problem; your fee is a rounding error.
- *customer_acquisition*: A free, personalised sample is a strong opener for creative services.
- *automation_ai*: Volume testing finds viral ads; AI makes 100x creative output possible.
- *scaling*: Unscalable services can be scaled with agents rather than hiring.
- *validation_testing*: Replicate outlier content that already worked.
- *scaling*: $10k/month via service is easy; $100k+ needs a product or owned audience.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Most AI influencer attempts fail from lack of consistency and creativity (survivorship bias).
- Consistency alone isn't enough without improving hooks and learning.
- AI ads can turn off sophisticated audiences in competitive categories.
- Target small companies; large ones hide the decision maker.
- UK note (extractor): AI-generated testimonials and benefits-claim ads risk ASA/CMA action; claims-management lead gen is FCA-regulated; cold LinkedIn/DM outreach only to limited-company decision makers under PECR.

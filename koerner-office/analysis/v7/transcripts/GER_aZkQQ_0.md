## Tactics and playbook steps

**Selfie-video Meta lead ads for home projects** (acquisition)
1. Record six unscripted selfie videos at the project site
2. Edit captions in CapCut (~5 minutes each)
3. Write headlines/primary text with ChatGPT
4. Run each ad in its own ad set at $100/day
5. Use a Meta instant form asking only name and phone
6. Push leads via Zapier to a Google Sheet with ad name
7. Score leads 1-10 and track close rate per ad; cut ads with high cost per lead
> "my leads are pushing through to a Google Sheet through Zapier" — Chris Koerner (~38:02)

**Pre-sell an event via newsletter form** (validation_testing)
1. Add a Google Form to your newsletter announcing a $100 Saturday AI seminar
2. Collect topic and length preferences
3. Book a venue only once ~100 sign up
4. Plan seminar and upsells with ChatGPT
> "I'll just have that link in my newsletter until I get to 100, and then that'll be the trigger" — Brandon (~22:21)

**Back-of-room upsell tiers** (sales)
1. Low-cost seminar ticket covers ads/venue
2. Offer one-time ~$1k packages for small firms
3. Offer $2-3k/month 12-month contracts for larger firms
4. Offer a $10k shadow-for-a-day then automate offer
5. Brand locally ('Minneapolis AI Experts')

**Niche community app ads** (acquisition)
1. Find large subreddits/Facebook groups for a passion hobby
2. Build a simple tracker app with AI coding
3. Run Reddit ads with a pain-point line that resonates

**Use BATNA framing in buyouts** (sales)
1. Identify the seller's alternative (e.g. a $500 dumpster)
2. Offer a quick cash buyout that beats it
3. Promise to clear by morning
> "So that's your that's your BATNA, your best alternative to a negotiated agreement" — Chris Koerner (~27:22)

**Lease-option to de-risk a land business** (finance)
1. Lease land on a busy road for 12 months
2. Agree a purchase price upfront
3. Prove the model and line up investors/banks during the lease

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Target die-hard hobby communities, especially in-person 'anti-AI' hobbies.
- *validation_testing*: Side features can become the main product (Slack started as a game chat).
- *offer_design*: Any investment thesis can be productised as a web app.
- *pricing*: Use a low-priced event to fund acquisition and profit on the high-ticket upsell.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Value-of-time arguments only hold if your time is fully optimised; doing it yourself can save $21k.
- *customer_acquisition*: Track ad performance through to closes, not just cost per lead.
- *validation_testing*: Pre-sell with a form before booking anything.
- *niche_selection*: Buy others' 'sawdust': leftovers people would pay to dump.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Social platforms can ban you; own your audience with email.
- Most leads ghost; that's normal.
- Stock screener is not investment advice (UK: FCA rules on advice/financial promotions).
- Ad spend can eat half of seminar ticket revenue.
- Local trust matters; don't look like a travelling salesman.

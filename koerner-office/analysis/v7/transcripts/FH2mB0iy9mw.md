A mindset-focused conversation between Chris Koerner and Shannon Gene (a reseller and content creator, speaking from Northern California) about the foundations of entrepreneurship: falling in love with problem solving, embracing fear and change, having 'delusions of grandeur' backed by action, and building a bias for action driven by curiosity and quick dopamine rewards. ## Tactics and playbook steps

**Validate demand cheaply with a local Facebook ad before spending** (acquisition)
1. Shoot a short video of the space
2. Edit it yourself to avoid friction
3. Create a Facebook ad campaign in about 10 minutes
4. Target a tight radius (six miles was chosen over five or seven)
5. Check cost per lead and ask leads follow-up questions like how often they'd play and how far they live
6. Extrapolate: stress-test at $10-20 per lead and 5% close rate
> "I need to put Facebook ads up to see if there's any demand for this." — Chris Koerner (~43:57)

**Learn manually before automating** (operations)
1. Do the task by hand first (e.g. follow people on Poshmark)
2. Observe which actions and buyer types lead to sales
3. Build a system from what you learned
4. Then automate it
> "we're going to learn how to do this manually. I'm going to teach you how to do it manually." — Shannon Gene (~20:28)

**Buy with math, sell with emotion** (finance)
1. Decide a maximum price from a spreadsheet
2. Enter the max bid in the auction software and walk away
3. Optionally use an odd number like 3505
4. Use emotional storytelling and video when selling
> "you need to buy with math and sell with emotion, right?" — Shannon Gene (~45:15)

**Whistle, workhorse and whale offer ladder** (pricing)
1. Create an attention-getting whistle offer
2. Make a workhorse offer that earns the daily bread-and-butter income
3. Add a whale high-end offer for fewer buyers
> "You you need a whistle for your business. What gets attention and then you get people in" — Shannon Gene (~11:55)

**Start reselling from your interests** (product)
1. Ask what the person likes (sports, camping, cooking, tools)
2. Find products in that interest, e.g. Blackstone grills
3. Find where to buy them and sell online
4. Use the early cash wins as motivation

**Act on curiosity within the hour** (mindset)
1. When a question or idea appears that can be answered in an hour, do it right away
2. Use notes and reminders apps for the rest
3. Look for a quick reward such as a first sale or lead

**Drop conversation hooks and ask questions** (sales)
1. Mention interesting things you have done and let people ask
2. Ask others about their business and how they make money
3. Connect more with people who ask questions back

## Lessons

- *mindset_execution*: Treat problems as the job, not as barriers; every business creates new problems, so expect and enjoy solving them.
- *validation_testing*: Start small and low-risk (e.g. selling household items) so early wins build confidence and a dopamine feedback loop.
- *validation_testing*: Validate demand with cheap tests before spending money, then stress-test the numbers with pessimistic assumptions.
- *mindset_execution*: Delusions of grandeur are useful if bolted onto action; ideas alone are bait, execution and tools are what matter.
- *automation_ai*: Learn the fundamentals by hand before automating or outsourcing; manual work reveals the system you later automate.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Keep businesses simple and focus on how much of each dollar reaches your pocket.
- *mindset_execution*: Change something when stuck: routine and habit lock in bad situations, so deliberately vary physical environment and routine.
- *niche_selection*: Niche, obscure products avoid competition and preserve margin compared with commodity goods.
- *mindset_execution*: Strategic ignorance of negative comments can help creators keep publishing consistently.
- *customer_acquisition*: Follow strategic people in business so your feed generates curiosity and ideas that prompt action.

## Warnings and mistakes

- If you stay in a bad rut and change nothing, nothing improves; change something physical or behavioural.
- Don't skip the manual stage and jump straight to automation or delegation; people who do never learn business fundamentals.
- Don't let emotion drive auction bids; stick to your pre-set maximum.
- You can ruin things you love by monetizing them.
- Don't talk too much about what you're going to do; talk about what you've done, or people see you as a blowhard.

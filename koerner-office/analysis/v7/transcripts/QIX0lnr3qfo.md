## Tactics and playbook steps

**Design the AI influencer for retention** (product)
1. Pick distinctive looks (unusual skin, eyes, scars) so viewers stall on first swipe
2. Avoid obviously non-human characters so the AI realisation is delayed a few seconds
3. Aim for high retention so short-form algorithms push the content wider
> "if I could delay that realization for just a few seconds, then it will make a massive difference." — Chris Koerner (~1:48)

**Hybrid human-plus-AI content** (product)
1. Record yourself performing the action or line on camera
2. Upload the recording to Higgsfield and select your AI influencer
3. Generate so the AI character mimics your performance, injecting your personality

**Monetise Instagram Reels through Higgsfield Earn** (acquisition)
1. Go to AI Influencer tab then Earn and connect Instagram or YouTube
2. Paste the emailed verification code in your bio and verify
3. Upload the video as a Reel with the caption 'Made in AI influencer studio on higgsfield.ai'
4. Copy the Reel link, submit under active campaigns with a PayPal email

**Drive traffic off-platform** (acquisition)
1. Use the page to build a newsletter or email list
2. Add affiliate links or sell digital products/courses

## Lessons

- *customer_acquisition*: Appearance and visual hook drive retention, which drives algorithmic distribution on short-form platforms.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Platform payouts per 1,000 views are small; treat them as a starting point and build an owned audience (email, products, affiliates).
- *finance_unit_economics*: Different platforms pay very differently: YouTube long-form and TikTok over-a-minute pay far more per view than Reels or Shorts.
- *automation_ai*: Use hybrid AI plus your own ideas rather than pure autopilot to stand out.
- *mindset_execution*: Learning new AI tools early is a low-cost way to learn how content and platforms work; you either win or learn.
- *customer_acquisition*: Tool vendors' affiliate/campaign programmes can create a payout where the platform itself does not pay (Instagram Reels).
- *scaling*: Low barriers to entry cut both ways: easier to start, but more competition, so speed matters.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Fully autopilot AI influencers are unlikely to succeed; human creativity still matters.
- Low barrier to entry means competition grows the longer you wait.
- Platform payouts alone rarely make serious money.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Niche-specific agency positioning** (acquisition)
1. Pick one industry you understand (here real estate)
2. Use industry language in outreach (speed to lead, MLS, pre-approval, commissions)
3. Reuse templates and case studies across clients
4. Stay in the niche until 5-10 retainer clients before expanding
> "positioning yourself as the AI person for every type of business, well, that forces you to create a different offer for every prospect." — Mikey (~3:12)

**Demo-led cold outreach with free audit** (sales)
1. Build three working demo agents first
2. Record a 2-3 minute Loom of lead arriving, fast reply, scoring and report
3. Email owner/managing broker directly: problem, system, measurable result
4. Offer a free 15-minute lead flow and operations audit rather than asking for $2,500
5. Avoid jargon like LLM orchestration or vector databases
6. Avoid generic info@ addresses
> "the strongest asset that you can bring into this outreach is a short recorded walkthrough of the demo agent." — Mikey (~33:57)

**Discovery call then single bundled package** (sales)
1. Ask them to walk through what happens the minute a lead comes in at 8pm
2. Ask hours per week spent sorting unqualified inquiries
3. Ask how weekly follow-up performance is tracked
4. Present all three agents as one package, not a menu
5. Document scope in a contract with max three active agents and full setup fee before configuration

**Standardised 48-hour onboarding** (operations)
1. Send secure OAuth credentials collection link, never passwords by email
2. Clone the three production-tested templates in Base44
3. Update prompts with company name, service area, hours, tone, rules
4. Replace demo connections with client's live accounts
5. Run end-to-end validation with simulated leads
6. Review with client then go live

**Case study to raise prices** (pricing)
1. Run the system 30 days
2. Capture response time, hours saved, extra showings
3. Use the case study in outreach
4. Raise setup fee from ~$1,500 to $3,500+

**Test agents with simulated leads before going live** (product)
1. Run a sample inquiry (name, property, budget) through the agent
2. Test two opposite leads to check scoring separation
3. Run a sample week of numbers for the report
4. Fix wording and criteria before touching a live inbox

## Lessons

- *offer_design*: Sell a specific expensive problem (slow lead response) with measurable ROI, not AI technology.
- *niche_selection*: Specialising in one niche makes outreach more credible and lets templates, case studies and processes be reused.
- *pricing*: Setup fee plus retainer beats hourly billing because efficiency raises rather than lowers your earnings.
- *customer_acquisition*: Lead with the strongest-ROI demo (speed to lead) in sales conversations.
- *offer_design*: Weekly reporting makes the retainer's value visible and reduces churn risk.
- *hiring_outsourcing*: Document every procedure so delivery can be handed to a junior operator and the founder can focus on sales.
- *scaling*: Productised, cloned templates make marginal delivery cost per client drop dramatically.
- *validation_testing*: Test with simulated inputs before connecting to live client systems.
- *automation_ai*: Treat low-code AI agent platforms as a dependency; the defensible value is niche knowledge, sales and process rather than the tool.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Do not undercharge for the first deal; very low prices attract clients expecting unlimited revisions.
- Do not start client-specific work on a verbal agreement; document scope and payment terms first.
- Promising ten automations creates scope creep and more things that can break; start with two or three.
- Do not expand to a new niche too early.
- Never ask clients to send passwords via email or chat; use OAuth.
- Do not ask for a $2,500 setup fee in the first cold message.

## Summary

Chris Koerner interviews Matt and Joanie, who started Sonoma Strong Hauling junk removal in Sonoma County when Matt was 35 and just out of jail and rehab. Starting part-time with a pickup and a free Craigslist post, they did $87k in year one at ~70% margin and now do $50k/month at ~35% net with a small crew. The episode focuses on free, old-school marketing: candy dishes at every real estate office, friending 2,000+ realtors on Facebook, daily local Facebook group posts, bandit signs, partner referrals and YouTube. Key takeaway: scrappy local relationship marketing plus professional branding can fill a low-capital service business, and growth spending should follow demand.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Candy dish realtor office drop** (acquisition)
1. Buy cheap glass dishes and nicer candy (e.g. Andes mints) at a dollar store
2. Order business cards from Vistaprint
3. List every real estate office per town via Google and split areas between partners
4. Walk in, introduce yourself to the front desk, leave the dish with cards at the desk or staff room
5. Repeat at storage facilities, apartment complexes and senior living
6. Keep spare dishes in the car for offices you pass
> "we would just put, you know, dollar store candy in there with our business cards around" — Joanie (~5:47)

**'Realtor stalker' social connecting** (acquisition)
1. Search Facebook for realtors in your county and add them as friends
2. Note open-house signs and friend the listing agent, or walk in and introduce yourself
3. Connect on Instagram and LinkedIn too
4. Post branded job photos regularly so they see you
> "I call myself a realtor stalker" — Matt (~13:36)

**Free local presence stack** (acquisition)
1. Free Craigslist labor-section post
2. Daily posts in local buy/sell, rentals and free-stuff Facebook groups
3. Bandit signs at busy intersections and near funeral homes/storage
4. Swap cards with movers, cleaners and handymen
5. Business name including the county for SEO
> "you can add yourself to a bunch of Facebook groups like buy now groups in your area, houses for rent in your area, free stuff in your area" — Matt (~14:39)

**Branded people-first photos** (acquisition)
1. Wear a branded shirt and hat from the first job
2. Post photos of the team smiling with the brand, not just junk piles
3. Tell the family-owned story in captions
4. Boost the best-performing posts
> "That's not going to make anybody stop and stop a scroll" — Joanie (~20:23)

**On-site sales sequence** (sales)
1. Show up professional with a printed price sheet
2. Compliment the house/dog/watch and build rapport
3. Walk the property
4. Only then give the price
> "You show up and you sell yourself first before you even give a price" — Matt (~27:40)

**Grow equipment only when demand forces it** (finance)
1. Start with pickup plus wooden side walls
2. Add utility trailer, then truck with dump trailer, then NPR dump trucks
3. Avoid financing new trucks before having the business
> "you want the market to tell you when to grow" — Matt (~28:36)

**Do marketing yourself first** (acquisition)
1. Build your own website and learn SEO
2. Post and update yourself to see what makes the phone ring
3. Hire an agency only once you know what works so you can hold them accountable

## Lessons

- *mindset_execution*: Constraints equal creativity: having no ad budget forced free, creative tactics.
- *customer_acquisition*: Target connectors who talk to many people (realtors, teachers, salons) to spread word of mouth.
- *mindset_execution*: Break overwhelming outreach into towns and daily quotas.
- *customer_acquisition*: Use multiple channels at once; big jobs came from ads, a realtor and a referral.
- *customer_acquisition*: Teaching your trade on YouTube can generate referrals from competitors and local SEO.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Stack revenue streams on one job: fee, resale, scrap.
- *mindset_execution*: Say yes to big intimidating jobs; they can make your year.
- *mindset_execution*: Just start; research paralysis stops people.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Underbidding: first job left only ~$100 after dump fees.
- Don't buy new trucks and trailers on finance after seeing hype videos before you have demand.
- Showing up in a plain t-shirt meant he couldn't charge enough.
- Outsourcing SEO/social from day one hides what is actually working.
- Platforms can ban you (host was banned from Facebook); own an email list.
- UK note (extractor): rubbish removal needs a waste carrier licence and duty-of-care transfer notes; roadside bandit signs need consent.

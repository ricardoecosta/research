## Summary

Side Hustle Nation host reviews Mediaprobe (mPanel in some countries), a media research panel that mails you a galvanic skin response sensor and pays gift cards for watching assigned TV shows while the sensor and your phone microphone record reactions. Entry is by application (he had a five-minute verification call), you get 5-10 session invites a month, and pay is about $5 per hour. He earned $10 from a MrBeast video and $30 from World Cup games, had one session denied for leaving early, and notes privacy concerns and gift-card-only payouts. Takeaway: fine for content you would watch anyway, but not a meaningful side income.

### I1. Paid TV-watching biometric research panel (Mediaprobe)

- **Customer:** Mediaprobe (the payer); its clients are TV networks and content companies
- **Problem:** Networks want to know how audiences really feel about content
- **Offer:** Viewer watches an assigned show at airtime wearing a sensor, with mic sync, then answers a short survey
- **Business model:** Panel participation paid per session in gift cards; research company sells reaction data to networks and brands.
- **Pricing:** Paid per session/hour of watch time. typical rate USD 5 per hour of watch time Paid only in gift cards (hundreds of options), sometimes next day.
- **Acquisition:** other: Viewer applies to the panel; Mediaprobe selects by demographics and viewing habits and may require a short call.
- **Delivery:** The panelist personally watches assigned shows at scheduled airtimes wearing the sensor; app uploads data afterwards.
- **Unit economics (claims):** About $5 per hour of watch time; 5-10 invites per month; $10 from one MrBeast video, $30 from World Cup games
- **Tools:** Mediaprobe app, Mediaprobe GSR sensor, Amazon gift cards
- **Startup cost:** Free (sensor mailed by the company)
- **Time to first revenue:** Paid as soon as the next day after a session
- **UK applicability [E]:** 3/5 — mPanel operates in other countries; UK availability not stated. Biometric data is special category data under UK GDPR, so consent terms matter.
- **Risks:** Biometric and always-on microphone privacy concerns; Gift-card-only payment; Cannot choose shows; must watch at airtime; Sessions denied if not completed in full; Tiny income, not scalable or automatable
- **Automation [E]:** acquisition 0/5 (Requires personal application and verification call.); onboarding 0/5 (Human must be approved and set up the sensor.); delivery 0/5 (A real person must physically watch with a biometric sensor attached; cannot be automated and faking it would be fraud.); billing 5/5 (Company pays gift cards automatically.); support 1/5 (Occasional emails to support for denied sessions.)

## Lessons

- *finance_unit_economics*: Get-paid-to apps trade time for very small pay; only worth it for activities you would do anyway.
- *niche_selection*: Data companies monetise consumer reactions and pass only a slice to the people producing the data; owning the data business is where the margin is.
- *operations*: Panels that ship hardware screen applicants hard to protect their per-panelist cost.
- *mindset_execution*: Your time is better spent on side hustles with higher earning potential than micro-task apps.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Mediaprobe requires microphone access during sessions to sync show audio, a privacy concern.
- Payouts are gift cards only, no cash, PayPal or Venmo.
- Leaving a session early gets it denied with no in-app warning.
- You cannot pick shows; only assigned shows at their airtime pay.

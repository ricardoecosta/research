## Tactics and playbook steps

**Friday handover / Monday reset** (operations)
1. Hand over asset Friday with condition photos and deposit
2. Collect and inspect Monday
3. Do the reset (clean, drain, wipe) before next weekend
4. Keep appointments to two a week
> "He calls it the Friday handover. Everything else is storage." — Narrator (~11:47)

**Deposit and photo protocol** (operations)
1. Hold a deposit via the booking platform
2. Take five photos (each corner plus key part) before the renter touches it
3. Inspect Monday in front of the renter and release the deposit
4. No deposit, no delivery, no exceptions
> "no deposit, no delivery, no exceptions" — Narrator (~11:13)

**Counter-seasonal asset pairing** (finance)
1. Start with a summer asset like a bounce house
2. Reinvest summer profit in a winter asset like a hot tub
3. Move inflatables indoors in autumn for halls and festivals

**Price at 3-5% of purchase price per day** (pricing)
1. Take purchase price of the tool
2. Charge 3-5% per day
3. Expect payback in about 20 rental days
> "You charge 3 to 5% of the purchase price per day." — Narrator (~6:32)

**Use insurance and paperwork as a moat** (legal)
1. Get general liability / certificate of insurance
2. Carry inspection tags and documentation
3. Target community, school and venue bookings that require paperwork

**Cultivate repeat commercial renters** (sales)
1. Identify small movers and contractors
2. Offer reliable availability
3. Aim for repeat bookings many times a year

## Lessons

- *finance_unit_economics*: Rental assets can pay back within a handful of bookings, so cheap used purchases cut risk.
- *legal_risk*: Insurance, paperwork and inspection requirements work as a moat against casual competitors.
- *scaling*: Pairing counter-seasonal assets smooths income across the year.
- *operations*: Choose assets by whether you can do the reset (cleaning, draining), as that is the real job people quit over.
- *scaling*: Staying small as a company of one is a strategy, avoiding fixed overheads.
- *validation_testing*: Idle assets you already own can be tested for rental value in 10 minutes of local price research.
- *customer_acquisition*: Repeat annual customers (families, event coordinators, cottage hosts) make bookings predictable.
- *automation_ai*: Low-handover assets like tools and trailers are fastest to start but physical work still caps automation.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Skipping deposit and condition photos led to damage with no leverage: about $180 loss and a lost booking.
- Check local rules; some places require a fire marshal or event safety tag for inflatables.
- Buying a seasonal asset out of season leaves it idle.
- Adding a shop and staff makes the weekend pay rent instead of profit.

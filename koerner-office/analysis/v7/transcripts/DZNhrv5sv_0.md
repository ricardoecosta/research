## Tactics and playbook steps

**Borrow against assets instead of selling** (finance)
1. Take a securities-backed line of credit against a portfolio
2. Keep LTV at 20-30% for stocks, under 10% for crypto
3. Pay interest from salary/business cash flow; ask CPA about deductibility
4. Avoid margin calls by not over-borrowing

**Structure a seller-financed acquisition** (finance)
1. Put ~10% down
2. Offer seller a higher total (e.g. $375k vs $349k ask) paid over 7 years
3. Pay seller a fixed share (40%) of profits until repaid
4. Skip the bank
> "you give the seller 40% of the profits until you pay back the 375 over these seven years" — Codie Sanchez (~15:09)

**Screen listings on cash flow vs price** (finance)
1. Compare asking price to cash flow
2. Check seasonality and hidden costs (e.g. captain)
3. Renegotiate or walk away

**Augusta rule (US)** (finance)
1. Rent your home to your business up to 14 days a year for meetings
2. Document properly; check with CPA

**Use business credit cards for float and rewards** (finance)
1. Pay in full monthly
2. Use 0% intro and cashback cards
3. Issue virtual cards with limits to staff (Ramp-style)
4. Pay off existing debt via snowball or avalanche

**Prioritise before extra mortgage payments** (finance)
1. Max retirement accounts
2. Kill debt above 10% interest
3. Build cash buffer
4. Then consider extra principal

## Lessons

- *finance_unit_economics*: Borrow to buy cash-producing, appreciating assets, not to consume.
- *exit_acquisition*: Leveraged acquisition of a cash-flowing business can far outperform index investing.
- *exit_acquisition*: Seller financing is common and lets you buy with less cash.
- *exit_acquisition*: Retiring boomers create a wave of businesses for sale.
- *exit_acquisition*: Increasing profit raises business value by the sale multiple.
- *finance_unit_economics*: A house is often a lifestyle purchase, not automatically an investment; compare total cost of owning.
- *legal_risk*: Never risk what you have and need for what you don't have and don't need.
- *scaling*: The goal is a business that runs without you.

## Warnings and mistakes

- SBA loans carry personal guarantees; failure lets the SBA pursue your house and assets.
- Over-borrowing against assets causes margin calls and forced sales at the worst time.
- Don't use 1:1 margin leverage.
- Carrying a credit card balance at ~22% turns float into disaster.
- Overpriced seasonal businesses (e.g. boat rental) can have negligible profit.
- Buying a business you don't know how to run, without due diligence, is the main way acquisitions fail.

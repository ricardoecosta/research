## Summary

Chris Koerner shows a beginner AI side hustle for garage door repair owners: build an instant quote generator and a job profit tracker with Lovable (no code), plus an after-hours AI voice answering agent in GoHighLevel. He scrapes about 513 Google Maps listings with Outscraper for $111, filters to mobile numbers, cold-texts 50 owners via Straight Text, and gets roughly 40-50% replies and one 'yes' within an hour. He pitches $50 per closed lead with no upfront cost and extrapolates to $100k/month, which is hypothetical. Key takeaway: lead with a low-risk offer, validate with real outreach before building, and use no-code/AI tools to ship fast. Lovable is a channel sponsor and the cold SMS method is illegal in the UK for sole traders.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Scrape, clean and mobile-filter a local lead list** (acquisition)
1. Use Outscraper's Google Maps scraper with the keyword and a handful of local cities, max 1,000 results, phone not blank
2. Export to Google Sheets and delete unneeded columns, keeping name, website, address and phone
3. Standardise phone numbers by removing +1 and dashes
4. Remove duplicates on phone number (multi-location companies)
5. Sort by line type and keep only confirmed mobile numbers
6. Use conditional formatting for 'repair' in the name to prioritise likely repair businesses
> "I'm only going to keep these cell phone numbers." — Chris Koerner (~22:09)

**Short validating first message** (sales)
1. Keep the first text under 160 characters
2. Only validate that they own the business first; don't pitch
3. Phrase as 'do you still fix...' to prompt a yes
4. Include your first name and local city
5. Add varied city names as a custom variable to vary the messages
6. Wait until all 50 are sent before replying
> "I'm just trying to validate that they own the business that I think they own first. We'll get to the sales pitch later." — Chris Koerner (~24:44)

**Low-risk launch offer** (pricing)
1. Offer no contracts, no upfront money and free setup
2. Charge only when a lead closes (e.g. $50)
3. Raise prices or add retainers later once demand is proven
> "I don't want the price to get in the way of the business being successful or not." — Chris Koerner (~28:37)

**Use ChatGPT to write the Lovable prompt and the voice agent prompt** (product)
1. Ask ChatGPT (thinking mode) for a thorough prompt tailored to Lovable, stating you are selling to garage door owners and want a mobile-friendly web app
2. Paste it into Lovable and follow the suggested follow-up prompts
3. Use ChatGPT to generate the voice agent knowledge base/script (background, instructions, rules, script)
4. Click 'fix' when Lovable's readiness checks fail
> "the best way to prompt a vibe coding app like lovable is to first ask ChatGBT to give you the best prompt for it for that specific tool." — Chris Koerner (~1:55)

**Choose a high-ticket, after-hours niche without private-equity competition** (acquisition)
1. List high-ticket home services that get after-hours calls
2. Skip plumbing, HVAC and electrical because private equity has them
3. Pick a niche like garage doors, where fixes cost $400-$2,500
4. Target local suburbs so you can sell as a local

**Urgency-based call forwarding workflow** (operations)
1. Give the client a dedicated or ported phone number
2. Create a GoHighLevel workflow that forwards the call only if the caller indicates urgency
3. Set secondary and tertiary numbers if the owner doesn't answer

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Validate demand with real outreach before building or investing; Chris built the voice agent only after finding interested owners, and a pitch tested live cost under $100.
- *pricing*: A risk-free launch offer (no upfront, no contract, pay per closed lead) removes price as the reason a test fails; raise prices later.
- *niche_selection*: Pick niches with high ticket sizes and urgent, after-hours demand that big players have not yet consolidated.
- *automation_ai*: Voice AI that took venture-funded startups to build can now be approximated in minutes with templated platforms, so the moat is distribution, not the tech.
- *automation_ai*: Use AI to write prompts for other AI tools; it saves time and gives more complete scoped builds.
- *customer_acquisition*: Locality helps close: use a local number, city name and your first name so buyers see you as local even when you never meet.
- *customer_acquisition*: Keep first messages short and ask a simple yes/no question, then pitch only after they reply.
- *finance_unit_economics*: The revenue extrapolation (200 customers x $500) assumes a 1% close rate from one tiny sample; treat it as illustration, not forecast.
- *mindset_execution*: Software builds are commoditised; the scarce skill is finding and closing customers, so spend most effort on selling.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Most deals never close; a single 'yes' reply does not mean revenue.
- Undifferentiated bulk messages risk iMessage blocks; personalise with city or name.
- Longer first texts split into two SMS and get lower responses.
- Landline and unknown line types were deleted to avoid risk when texting.
- UK note (analyst): unsolicited marketing texts to sole traders and individuals breach PECR; the method does not transfer directly.

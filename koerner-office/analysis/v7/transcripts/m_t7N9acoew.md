## Tactics and playbook steps

**Find businesses with visible website problems** (acquisition)
1. Search for a local service and area, such as fence repair in Miami.
2. Prioritize small or family-owned businesses with outdated, text-heavy, or malfunctioning websites.
3. Check whether the business has social pages and inspect the site’s quote forms, service pages, images, and contact information.
> "So, for this one, what we're going to do is fence repair. And since I just literally moved to Florida, I'm going to do fence repair Miami." — George McConnell (~1:48)

**Assemble a complete business-specific brief** (operations)
1. Collect the business name, phone number, website, social links, domain, service areas, core services, offers, and required legal lines.
2. Reuse existing image URLs only after checking that they are suitable and permitted.
3. Specify brand positioning, voice, colors, typography, page layouts, navigation, lead capture, and technical SEO.
4. Include page-by-page requirements, copy rules, and explicit instructions to avoid placeholders and generic layouts.
> "This is important because you have to actually kind of go over the website and see all right this is what's important to them and pull that and extract that information out and have that info in there." — George McConnell (~9:47)

**Give the AI a detailed build task and let it run** (operations)
1. Attach the complete brief and select the website-building task.
2. Tell the AI to read the entire brief, follow every section, use the real image URLs, and not ask clarifying questions.
3. Tell it to make sensible choices wherever the brief leaves details open.
4. Review the finished pages, links, and forms before presenting the demo.
> "Do not summarize the brief. This is important because sometimes, you know, when you're using these things like Fable, CHBT, Asher, things like that, they kind of want to re-edit it for some reason." — George McConnell (~13:16)

**Sell the result by showing the demo** (sales)
1. Build the prospect’s demo before contacting them.
2. Send the link directly to the owner and explain how the new site could help customers find and contact the business.
3. Ask for the website project if the owner likes the demo.
4. Offer a monthly retainer to keep the website running smoothly; the amount is not stated.
> "Grab that link, send it to them, say, "Listen, I built you a new website. you are missing out on money. People cannot find you. People cannot see you. People cannot contact you." — George McConnell (~25:06)

**Treat outreach as a volume activity** (acquisition)
1. The speaker suggests sending demos to 10 or 20 prospects each day.
2. Track replies and sales rather than assuming every prospect will buy.
3. Check that each outreach method is lawful for the business type and recipient.
> "This type of service in this industry is a numbers game. You can't just think that you're going to find one small business every single day and they're going to immediately say, "Yes, build me a website."" — George McConnell (~24:45)

## Lessons

- *other*: Show a prospect a tailored result to make the offer concrete, rather than relying on a generic claim that you can build websites.
- *offer_design*: Look for problems that affect a business’s ability to capture leads, such as broken quote forms or hard-to-find contact details.
- *automation_ai*: A detailed brief can give an AI tool enough context to create a more consistent result across pages.
- *operations*: Build a repeatable process around collecting business details, defining the design, and specifying every page and form.
- *finance_unit_economics*: A website can support recurring revenue if the customer also pays for ongoing maintenance.
- *customer_acquisition*: Do not assume a single prospect will buy; the speaker recommends consistent outreach volume.
- *offer_design*: Use customer-specific content and verify the details that matter to the business instead of publishing generic placeholder copy.
- *validation_testing*: Treat AI output as a draft that needs checks; the speaker himself spots a mismatch in the displayed fence-system count.

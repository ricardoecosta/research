## Tactics and playbook steps

**Focus on a short list of high-impact prospects** (acquisition)
1. Define the outcome you want, such as a client, mentor, or partner.
2. Build a small list of people who could materially change the outcome.
3. Spend time researching and approaching that list rather than defaulting to a large generic campaign.
> "what happened if we took 5 hours trying to reach like the 20 most influential people that could completely transform our business?" — Chris Koerner (~3:44)

**Make outreach specific to the recipient** (acquisition)
1. Research the recipient's public interests and current context.
2. Choose a relevant detail that makes the approach feel specific to them.
3. Make the recipient come out well in any humor or creative work.
> "So, the cartoon always going to be about the recipient. And because of what cartoons do in magazines and newspapers anyway" — Stu Heinik (~19:02)

**Use a physical teaser to make the recipient open the mail** (acquisition)

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: A focused list of people who can change the outcome may be more useful than a large list of weakly targeted prospects.
- *customer_acquisition*: Make an approach distinctive and relevant to the person receiving it; generic outreach is easy to ignore.
- *customer_acquisition*: Personalization works best when it reflects a real interest or current circumstance, not a superficial detail.
- *other*: Treat outreach as an audition for a working relationship; the recipient is judging what it would be like to work with you.
- *offer_design*: If a prospect responds, stay flexible about the problem they need solved instead of forcing the original offer.
- *customer_acquisition*: A memorable creative idea can spread beyond the people originally targeted.
- *automation_ai*: Use AI to speed up research and customization, but the guest believes practiced human creative work can still stand out.
- *other*: Follow-through matters after a strong first impression; make the next step easy and match the prospect's immediate need.
- *offer_design*: A creative approach should make the prospect's next action clear, whether that is opening a mailpiece, taking a call, or agreeing to a trial.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Soft sell / play hard to get with buyers** (sales)
1. Never push hard sales in a scam-prone industry
2. Call buyers casually: 'you may not be interested, but...'
3. Mention other buyers to create gravity
4. Underpromise and overdeliver
> "You got to use like gravity to try to get them to come to you." — Shannon Jean (~1:09)

**Find wholesale buyers via eBay** (acquisition)
1. Search every eBay seller in the target category
2. Reach out offering weekly supply
3. Serious buyers will fly out to inspect
4. Take only a few percent margin so the buyer wins

**Validate before buying with eBay sold prices and test listings** (product)
1. Filter eBay by Sold (green prices), not completed
2. Compare to buy price and fees
3. List on Facebook Marketplace with Google images at target profit
4. Wait an hour, check clicks, then buy

**Always get a manifest; start small** (operations)
1. Request a spreadsheet manifest of every item
2. Avoid no-manifest loads unless sophisticated
3. Start with a few pallets, not a truckload
4. Use Costco/B-Stock for good problem resolution
> "One of the most important things about buying this stuff is to you always want to get a manifest" — Shannon Jean (~8:16)

**Earn trust by overpaying when deals are worth more** (sales)
1. When a deal is worth more than the seller thinks, pay them extra
2. Word spreads; deals start coming to you
> "You were gonna make $100,000, but you know what? It's actually worth a lot more. I'm going to pay you 150." — Shannon Jean (~26:28)

**Finance big deals with a buyer in hand** (finance)
1. Line up buyer and product
2. Borrow hard money at 10-12% or negotiate time with a trusted supplier
3. Flip quickly to repay
> "if the deal is good enough, you could go get the money" — Shannon Jean (~27:51)

## Lessons

- *finance_unit_economics*: Profits in reselling come from averages over time, not every deal.
- *customer_acquisition*: Let partners make the bulk of the money to earn repeat volume.
- *scaling*: Credibility and relationships bring inbound deal flow and financing.
- *validation_testing*: Test whether you like entrepreneurship by selling things around your house; risk is only time.
- *mindset_execution*: Frustration is a feature that keeps competitors out.
- *niche_selection*: Invert: find the most valuable part of a commodity that others miss.
- *operations*: The system matters more than the product; it takes a year to a year and a half to build.
- *mindset_execution*: Category matters less than taking action; fewer than 1% of followers act.
- *pricing*: A story (scratch and dent) lets buyers rationalise a deal that otherwise looks like a scam.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Buying without a manifest can leave you with no recourse.
- Store returns (air fryers, Blackstones) are dirty and need cleaning.
- Don't compare your profit to your buyer's; it breeds needless doubt.
- Losing money on the first deal kills beginners' belief; ask experienced resellers first.
- Scammers use the same soft-sell tactics; vet counterparties.
- Scratch-and-dent and big items need logistics beyond a garage.
- UK: resale of used electricals and upholstered furniture is subject to safety regulations; marketplace sellers are reported to HMRC.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Clone a high-converting site with Readdy** (product)
1. Google 'beautiful home service business websites' and pick one you like
2. In Readdy, prompt: copy the design from <domain> but for <business>, services, service area cities, include a quote form
3. Ask for urgency elements (timer, coupon, promo tag) in the hero
4. Swap AI images for real project photos
5. Check mobile version
6. Configure the AI chat and voice agent prompts; test that bookings land in Appointments
7. Publish and connect a domain
> "Copy the design from kitchenc cabinet.net. But the new website needs to be for a business called Backyard Funhouse" — Chris Koerner (~3:10)

**Copy the business practices of well-optimised competitors** (offer_design)
1. If a high-converting site offers financing, offer financing
2. Keep phone number persistent in the header
3. Copy menu structure and multiple calls to action

**A/B test agency pricing through outreach** (pricing)
1. Offer one prospect a flat fee
2. Offer another $100/month
3. Offer a third a combo
4. Keep the price the market accepts

**Become GC on high-ticket jobs using subcontractors** (operations)
1. Build your own project with subcontractors
2. Keep the rolodex
3. Sell the same service to others at retail

## Lessons

- *pricing*: Charge for the value created, not the time or effort.
- *niche_selection*: Most home service owners don't know AI can build sites in minutes, so the knowledge gap is the margin.
- *offer_design*: A well-optimised competitor site reveals their business practices (financing, persistent phone number) worth copying.
- *validation_testing*: AI site builders increase the speed of testing; test several landing pages before committing.
- *niche_selection*: Move up-market: compare ticket sizes ($1,500 tree jobs vs $50-60k sport courts).
- *automation_ai*: Low-latency AI voice agents can book appointments directly into a calendar.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Using images scraped from Google Images risks copyright problems; AI images or your own photos avoid that.
- Charging too much loses deals; test pricing.
- Cold outreach to sole-trader tradespeople is restricted in the UK under PECR (consent needed for email/SMS); only limited companies can be cold emailed.

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Check the exit price before buying inventory: the host compares local offers with the online route before deciding where to sell.
- *offer_design*: A marketplace can add trust to a high-value transaction by checking authenticity and holding funds until validation.
- *operations*: Protect high-value shipments with careful packing, photos and receipts.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Reward calculations depend on card terms and merchant categorization, so the headline percentage may not apply to every buyer.
- *customer_acquisition*: Inventory availability can change quickly; the employee advises checking stock early and treating it as first come, first served.
- *finance_unit_economics*: The host's own example separates a proposed margin from realized profit: he ships the bar but is still waiting for payment.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Pick the calculator niche with Google Trends** (product)
1. Shortlist two candidate calculators
2. Compare them side by side in Google Trends over 5 years
3. Build the one with growing search interest
4. Favour a niche you know and like
> "So, what I'm going to do is go to Google Trends and I'm going to put these two finalists for my calculator side by side and see which one wins." — Chris Koerner (~6:45)

**Find supply and demand imbalances in keywords** (acquisition)
1. Use Semrush, Ahrefs or an AI tool to list core and long-tail keywords
2. Look for terms with higher volume but fewer competitors
3. Target rising trends such as AI, GLP-1s, solar, protein and side hustles
> "Which term or terms have more volume but fewer competitors?" — Chris Koerner (~5:20)

**Gate results behind an email** (acquisition)
1. Prompt the builder to blur results until an email is entered
2. Test that the gate works, since the first build leaked results
3. Use the emails for a newsletter and affiliate links
> "don't display the result for the end user until I get their email address" — Chris Koerner (~7:32)

**Iterate with screenshots and the AI helper** (product)
1. Screenshot the part to change and reprompt
2. Make wording simple for laymen
3. Add a token counter tool for engagement
4. Add tips content at the bottom for SEO
5. Ask the helper assistant (Cody) to write a better fix prompt when something breaks

**Clone the template into more niches** (operations)
1. Use a domain name with a plural such as calculators
2. Copy the calculator and swap its functions for a new niche
3. Use the starter plan's up to 25 sites

**Layer monetisation beyond AdSense** (finance)
1. Enable AdSense first with a one-line prompt
2. Then add other ad tools
3. Collect emails and monetise them via a newsletter or affiliate offers

## Lessons

- *mindset_execution*: Cheap, fast experiments suit small web tools: the downside is a few dollars and a few hours, so ship more and overthink less.
- *validation_testing*: Validate demand with search-trend data before building, not by guessing.
- *automation_ai*: Single-purpose utility sites can earn from traffic with no customers, sales or support, which makes them well suited to agent-run operations.
- *niche_selection*: Ride rising trends (AI, GLP-1s, solar, protein, side hustles) where supply of tools lags demand.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Capture emails as well as ad revenue, since a subscriber list can be worth far more than AdSense per visitor (a hypothetical claim).
- *customer_acquisition*: Pair calculators with a lead-gen directory: a calculator can pull in traffic and capture leads that are sold to providers.
- *niche_selection*: Choose a niche you know and love so the project is more sustainable.
- *offer_design*: Use the sunk-cost effect carefully: the more effort a user invests before the email ask, the more likely they are to give it.

## Warnings and mistakes

- AdSense alone earns little; do not stop there. Without ads the site never monetises.
- Vibe-coded builds will have hang-ups; test that the email gate actually works.
- The host says the opportunity may disappear in 2 to 5 years, and older calculators can lose traffic.
- Omni Calculator's scale is not a realistic expectation.

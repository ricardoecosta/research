## Lessons

- *customer_acquisition*: Make the first customer action easy: the home-service ad sends people to online appointment scheduling instead of requiring a phone call.
- *operations*: When using subcontractors, test reliability on simpler work before assigning higher-risk jobs.
- *hiring_outsourcing*: Transparent pricing with subcontractors can help maintain repeat working relationships.
- *customer_acquisition*: A good service outcome can create referrals and reduce the need for constant new-customer marketing.
- *validation_testing*: A solo instructor can test local demand with simple offline promotion and referrals before investing in SEO or a website.
- *scaling*: Turn an instructor's waiting list into a product or format that does not require every customer to receive one-to-one time.
- *customer_acquisition*: Repeat customers in a local service business can come from building relationships with businesses that need the service regularly.
- *offer_design*: A course can combine self-paced material with paid access to an expert when students need help.
- *niche_selection*: Specialised equipment can limit competition, but it also creates a substantial capital requirement.
- *scaling*: A franchise can provide a ready-made curriculum, training, software, and marketing, while a focused single location avoids expanding too quickly.
- *pricing*: Doing free work can build skill and trust, but the operator should connect that experience to a clear paid offer.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Recurring upkeep can turn one-off website projects into ongoing subscription revenue.

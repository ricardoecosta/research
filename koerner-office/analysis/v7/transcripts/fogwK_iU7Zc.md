## Summary

Chris Koerner explains his short-form-video-to-newsletter funnel: post many front-facing-camera short videos with a strong call to action, convert roughly 0.02% of views to Beehive newsletter subscribers, and treat each post as a near-free lottery ticket. He shares his stats (15-30k organic subscribers per month, 30M views per week), his workflow with a cheap overseas editor and VA, and the tracking KPIs (views per follow, followers per day). A Q&A covers editor hiring, LinkedIn, a 60-videos-in-30-days plan, CTAs and AI scripting. Key takeaway: volume of publishing, retention-chart iteration and CTAs matter more than polish.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Open-loop hook plus answer-in-newsletter CTA** (acquisition)
1. Use a visual, verbal or text hook that opens a question
2. Keep the loop open until the end or until the CTA
3. Put the CTA early (about 2.5 seconds) and again at the end
4. Answer the video's question in the newsletter welcome email
> "You have to answer the question in your newsletter that your short form video question asks or they have no reason to follow you." — Chris Koerner (~27:24)

**TikTok-first retention testing** (acquisition)
1. Post to TikTok first because it is the least valuable audience
2. Wait 1-2 hours and inspect the retention chart
3. If flat, publish everywhere; if drop-offs, cut weak sentences or move/remove the CTA
4. Upload manually to each of the five platforms, no cross-posting
> "I post my video, and when I say I, I mean my team to Tik Tok first, and we wait 1 to two hours" — Chris Koerner (~13:20)

**Idea-to-business-plan SEO flywheel** (operations)
1. Spot a business idea while scrolling reels
2. Film an unscripted front-camera video
3. Assistant prompts a custom Claude assistant to generate a business plan
4. Use ahrefs to pick keywords and publish the plan on the website
5. Give subscribers a link to search the plans

**Train the Instagram algorithm for ideas** (product)
1. Reset Instagram recommendations
2. Like, bookmark and comment on posts with great hooks in your niche
3. Within about 30 minutes the feed shows inspiration to emulate

**Track two KPIs with a VA** (operations)
1. Track views per follow and followers per day per platform and per piece
2. Have a VA update the sheet
3. Use it to judge CTA strength

**Recycle bangers** (operations)
1. Re-film a viral video every 3 months
2. Have it re-edited so it does not look identical to the algorithm

**LinkedIn link-adding trick** (acquisition)
1. Publish lots of content with no links
2. When a post performs above average, edit the post a few hours later to add the link in the post body
3. Favour PDFs/large images that require zooming

**AI scripting from competitor transcripts** (product)
1. Scrape transcripts of niche videos with Apify
2. Feed into ChatGPT to rewrite with a better hook and payoff
3. Give 5 example ideas and ask for 50 more

## Lessons

- *mindset_execution*: Treat each post as a cheap lottery ticket: low cost per attempt means volume is the best strategy.
- *customer_acquisition*: A strong CTA improves follower conversion dramatically (about 300-800 views per follow versus 1,000-5,000 without) at little cost to reach.
- *scaling*: Follower count still matters because more data points let algorithms target your content, raising baseline views 10-20x.
- *automation_ai*: Authentic human faces will stand out as AI slop proliferates.
- *customer_acquisition*: Own the audience on a newsletter platform rather than renting it on social platforms.
- *hiring_outsourcing*: Do the work by hand for 200-300 videos before outsourcing so you understand what good looks like.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Value subscribers by summing a 1% purchase assumption across possible products, cross-checked against industry ad CPM benchmarks.
- *niche_selection*: Pick a niche you can talk about forever and never run out of content in.
- *validation_testing*: Iterate using retention charts: cut weak lines and relocate the CTA.
- *hiring_outsourcing*: Cheap overseas editors and a VA make high-volume content affordable.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Auto-generated podcast clips rarely go viral; front-facing original videos work better.
- Audience from another platform (e.g., Twitter) does not transfer to short-form video.
- Do not jump straight to a paid-offer CTA; it won't convert, so massage the funnel step by step.
- Over-researching hooks and competitors is procrastination.
- CapCut terms of service changes made it unattractive.

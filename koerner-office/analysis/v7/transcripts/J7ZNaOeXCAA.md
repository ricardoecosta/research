## Tactics and playbook steps

**Pitch local TV news with a packaged human-interest angle** (acquisition)
1. Get first customers and a result worth talking about first
2. Fill out the station's online news tip form or text a contact
3. State what you did, the angle, and why viewers get value
4. Avoid sounding like a free commercial
5. Expect same-day filming (25-30 min interview)
6. Follow up months later with new milestones bundled together
7. Build a personal PR network of reporters
> "Here's the angle. Here's why other people would like to hear that." — John Cheney (~4:29)

**Leverage a partnership into a press release** (acquisition)
1. Land a credible partner (BYU athletics)
2. Ask a big company (Replit) to contribute (6 months free per athlete)
3. Get approved quotes from each party (can take weeks)
4. Distribute via PR Newswire (~$1,000)
5. Gain backlinks and pickup from Yahoo News, MSNBC, TechCrunch

**Cold email reporters from bylines** (acquisition)
1. Find relevant articles and the writer's byline email
2. Send a one-line pitch of your story from Gmail
3. Email several reporters; a few respond
4. One article can cascade to others
> "I bought one of everything, a buckies and I'm going to sell them on a website. Do you want to write about that?" — Chris Koerner (~37:37)

**30-day public gratitude posting challenge** (acquisition)
1. Post something you're grateful for every day for 30 days (John did 366)
2. Include at least one real failure you're grateful for
3. Optionally add a call to action asking followers what they're grateful for
> "Every single day posts something you're grateful for. That's it." — John Cheney (~23:43)

**Jab, jab, jab, right hook content** (acquisition)
1. Post free value daily (reviews of AI tools, what you like/dislike)
2. Act like a reporter rather than selling
3. Occasionally make an ask

**Post as a whole person on every platform** (acquisition)
1. Mix personal life (kayaking, family, piano) with work content
2. On LinkedIn, tie personal posts to professional lessons
3. Personal content raises DM response rates even with few followers

## Lessons

- *mindset_execution*: People fear others' perception of failure, not failure itself.
- *customer_acquisition*: Local news desks often have no backlog and want ready-made stories.
- *customer_acquisition*: Do the reporter's job for them: package the story and the viewer benefit.
- *offer_design*: Sell the future desired state, not features.
- *customer_acquisition*: Platforms run on interest graphs; consistent topical posting finds the right audience.
- *customer_acquisition*: Earned media is an asymmetric bet: one email can be worth millions.
- *mindset_execution*: Build in public; ideas are cheap, execution is what counts.
- *mindset_execution*: False constraints (build a business in one day) can force action.
- *customer_acquisition*: Showing up in person can itself be the story angle.
- *scaling*: Borrow credibility from bigger names via partnerships.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Media won't give you a free commercial; pitches must be human interest or inspiring.
- Don't spam reporters; only pitch with real news.
- Telling stories about how awesome you are backfires; relate to the audience instead.
- Social media takes years of daily consistency; quitting after two months is too early.
- Day trading consumed three months and lost $10k.
- Press releases need approved quotes from partners and can take weeks.
- UK: Playmakers teaches cold SMS/calls; in the UK cold SMS to individuals/sole traders breaches PECR and calls must be TPS/CTPS screened.
- Transcript is Whisper tiny: names (John Cheney, company names) may be mis-heard.

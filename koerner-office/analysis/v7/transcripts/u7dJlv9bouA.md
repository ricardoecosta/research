## Tactics and playbook steps

**Use deep research for structured desk research** (operations)
1. Give the research tool a specific task and name the sources or websites to inspect.
2. Ask it to return findings in a defined format, such as a table.
3. For large datasets, state the number of rows and require the full dataset rather than a sample.
4. Ask for concise bullet points after receiving a long report.
5. Check key claims against an independent source such as Google Trends before treating them as evidence.
> "I want you to find me like unique foods in other countries that are starting to take on here in the United States" — Chris Koerner (~6:51)

**Give AI explicit research instructions** (operations)
1. Describe the input data, its size, and the fields in each row.
2. Specify the output fields, categories, and constraints.
3. Explain how to score or rank results.
4. Follow up to clarify gaps rather than assuming the first response is complete.

## Lessons

- *automation_ai*: Use AI where it already performs well, such as research and synthesis, instead of assuming every task needs a fully autonomous agent.
- *operations*: Give AI clear goals, context, constraints, and examples; vague delegation can produce the wrong work.
- *operations*: For large datasets, state the expected number of records and specify that the full dataset should be processed.
- *validation_testing*: Use an independent source to corroborate AI-generated research before pursuing a business idea.
- *niche_selection*: Look for opportunities in products and behaviours that are popular elsewhere but less established in your target market.
- *validation_testing*: A portable version of an entertainment concept can be a lower-commitment way to test demand than opening a permanent venue.
- *mindset_execution*: A simple retrofit or distribution approach can be enough to test a business; avoid assuming that every opportunity needs complex invention.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Validate demand, operational capacity, and financing before committing to large orders or debt.
- *automation_ai*: AI can lower the cost of gathering information, but people still need to interpret evidence and decide what it means.
- *mindset_execution*: New technology may take a long time to reach dependable real-world use, even when its potential is clear.

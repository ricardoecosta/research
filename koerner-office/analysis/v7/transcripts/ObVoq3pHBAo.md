## Lessons

- *scaling*: Diversifying across several companies in a holding structure reduces emotional volatility because they rarely all go badly at once.
- *mindset_execution*: Do not get emotionally tied to any one business result; losses at one company are offset by growth at another.
- *mindset_execution*: When things go badly, take it on the chin and make the needed decision, but protect balance through health, family and friends.
- *hiring_outsourcing*: Hire great people to sit between you and day-to-day problems, but expect to own the biggest problems yourself.
- *mindset_execution*: Seek out constructive criticism and weigh it by the source's closeness and credibility.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Running large companies is not glamorous; with investor money and employees, the big problems always come to the owner even with strong managers in between.
- Becoming arrogant and believing you know everything is a key risk as you grow.

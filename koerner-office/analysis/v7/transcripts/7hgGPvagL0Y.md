## Tactics and playbook steps

**Generous refund policy to protect reputation** (operations)
1. If a cleaner clearly underperformed, offer a discount or full refund without arguing
2. Handle case by case, but don't keep money from unhappy clients
> "I'm not afraid to offer a discount or refund" — Luis Garcia (~9:33)

**Review request only after confirming happiness** (acquisition)
1. Follow up after each clean asking how it went
2. Ask for a review only if the client is truly happy
3. Never offer incentives for reviews

**Vet contractor cleaners** (hiring)
1. Interview on camera and check online reviews
2. Always run a test clean before a real job
3. Keep recruiting continuously to build a backup pipeline of cleaners, including specialists (post-construction)

**Google Business Profile basics** (acquisition)
1. Maintain GBP actively
2. Upload geotagged photos
3. Invest in local SEO and social media

**Hire experienced VAs to run operations** (hiring)
1. Hire VAs with cleaning-industry experience who can sell and close
2. Let them handle complaints so the owner can keep a day job
3. Over-communicate with clients

## Lessons

- *customer_acquisition*: Don't keep money from unhappy customers in a subjective B2C service; it protects reviews.
- *hiring_outsourcing*: Treat cleaner recruiting as a continuous pipeline like a sales funnel.
- *hiring_outsourcing*: Get the business to run on VAs before returning to a job so you can double-dip.
- *customer_acquisition*: Do the basics well: GBP, local SEO and social presence beat word of mouth for scale.
- *mindset_execution*: Get obsessive about the business once you commit.
- *mindset_execution*: Care about clients and contractors, not only money.
- *pricing*: Recurring deep-clean clients pay well and let you pay cleaners well.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Skipping a test clean led to a cleaner no-show on the first job.
- Even good contractors can ghost; without a bench of specialists the job must be cancelled.
- Not tracking numbers: owner does not know his actual profit.
- Complaints affect founders emotionally; delegate to VAs with a process.
- Episode is hosted by a cleaning-business coaching program promoting its course.

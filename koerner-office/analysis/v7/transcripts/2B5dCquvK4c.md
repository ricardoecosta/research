## Tactics and playbook steps

**Validate rental demand by scraping occupancy** (product)
1. Search RVshare/Outdoorsy (or Airbnb glamping) for this weekend and a year out
2. Log availability by type in a spreadsheet
3. Buy the type/market with highest occupancy

**Hero workflow selection** (product)
1. Pain: clear operational cost/stress
2. Access: who can you reach
3. Infrastructure: uses some digital tools, not too many
4. Dollar savings: real savings
5. Pick high-impact, low-complexity, magic-feeling workflows; one per niche
> "you're looking for hero workflows. And these are what hero workflows are. They're high impact and they're low complexity" — Nick (~26:01)

**Live AI receptionist demo outreach** (acquisition)
1. Build GoHighLevel AI voice agent with ChatGPT knowledge base
2. Pick an un-saturated higher-ticket niche with after-hours calls (garage doors)
3. Scrape every company in the metro, randomise
4. Message offer: pay only when a lead closes

**Dormant newsletter sourcing** (acquisition)
1. Use AI agents to list newsletters on Beehiiv/Kit/Substack directories with no send in a month
2. Offer pennies per subscriber or contingency/seller finance
3. Aggregate in one niche; sell CPM ads; convert weekly to daily

**Lease with option to validate land businesses** (finance)
1. Lease raw land (e.g. $3k/month) with option to buy in a year
2. Install business (storage, glamping)
3. Buy only after validation

**Pre-sell events with ads** (acquisition)
1. Run targeted ads for an event before buying gear
2. Refund if not enough tickets

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Validate rental demand with marketplace data before buying assets.
- *validation_testing*: Learn by building first, then research and market.
- *customer_acquisition*: Piggyback on people who already have customer networks.
- *niche_selection*: Large companies waste huge sums on manual processes; domain experts can find them.
- *niche_selection*: Most small businesses don't answer after-hours calls; AI receptionists are a blue ocean.
- *niche_selection*: Unbundle a pain point big companies hate doing (coil cleaning, stump grinding).
- *offer_design*: Packaging can turn a boring activity into a premium experience.
- *exit_acquisition*: Equity for value-add can beat agency fees when you can't afford to buy.
- *mindset_execution*: Short focused intense build periods can buy long-term freedom.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Self-managing a single rental asset has no scale and becomes annoying.
- The last 10% of vibe-coded apps is still the hardest part.
- Companies get squeamish about percentage-of-savings pricing; prefer predictable fees.
- Always 'just a few more months' of grinding can become permanent.
- Plumbing/HVAC/electrical are saturated with PE and existing AI answering.

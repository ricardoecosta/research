## Tactics and playbook steps

**Daily agent SEO loop** (acquisition)
1. Each morning an agent queries Google Search Console/Keyword Planner via MCP tools
2. Find keywords with impressions but no clicks (e.g. 16,000 impressions at position ~50)
3. Write one high-quality guide targeting that keyword
4. Publish and add internal links from existing high-traffic pages
5. Wait 3-4 days for indexing and watch traffic and conversions
> "it runs through keyboard planner every single morning, looks at all the traffic, it identifies keywords that are being surfaced, but not essentially being driven to the site" — Rastie Turek (~7:46)

**Place CTAs where readers stop** (acquisition)
1. Have agents analyse where readers commonly end in an article
2. Put App Store CTAs at those points
3. Track web-to-app conversion (~10%)
> "wherever people are like, commonly ending, I want them to be able to go through the app" — Rastie Turek (~7:08)

**Attack neglected incumbents' web keywords** (acquisition)
1. Pick a proven App Store category whose incumbents are 15+ years old
2. Accept you cannot outrank them in the App Store
3. Capture the same intent on the open web with guides
> "Most of the apps in this area are 15 years or older. They have millions of users" — Rastie Turek (~6:12)

**Monetise to filter feature requests** (pricing)
1. Launch free with a few paid advanced features
2. Offer weekly, annual and lifetime tiers
3. Expect conversion to rise over 30-60 days for utility apps
> "if users are sending me separate requests, I just don't want to be bothered as much" — Rastie Turek (~1:58)

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Proven, simple app categories with neglected incumbents are winnable; no new idea needed.
- *customer_acquisition*: SEO still works for niche intent when content is high quality and internally linked.
- *pricing*: Utility apps can convert late (day 35-60) unlike hype apps that must convert on day one.
- *automation_ai*: Coding agents let a domain outsider ship a paid iOS app in weeks.
- *finance_unit_economics*: A one-person app with free tooling can run with near-zero costs and nothing to break.
- *pricing*: Lifetime pricing can dominate revenue for privacy/utility apps.
- *mindset_execution*: Get better slightly every day rather than overthinking how things should be done.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Avoid AI 'slop' volume; he publishes only one high-quality guide a day.
- True end-to-end encryption means no recovery and can draw law-enforcement requests.
- Unlaunched projects don't exist; starting is the hardest part.
- Whisper transcript: some numbers/names garbled (intro '$23,000 a day' vs stated 2,000-3,000/day).

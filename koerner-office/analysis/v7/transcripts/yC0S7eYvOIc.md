### I1. AI content clipping (Whop / Vyro-style clipping campaigns)

- **Pricing:** Per view or per accepted clip, set by the campaign.  No rates stated.
- **Acquisition:** marketplace_platform: Find an active clipping campaign on a platform like Whop or Viral; watch at least three winning clips in the niche, then post the first clip within 48 hours.
- **Delivery:** Solo person edits clips using basic video editors (e.g. a tap cutter) and posts to TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- **Unit economics (claims):** Startup cost close to zero; Scored 7/25, the fastest time to first dollar of the seven
- **Tools:** Whop, Viral, video editor
- **Startup cost:** Close to zero
- **Time to first revenue:** Shortest of the seven; no specific time stated
- **UK applicability [E]:** 4/5 — Platforms are global; check payout and tax handling (self-assessment) for the UK, and platform terms.
- **Risks:** Campaign payouts and availability depend on the platform; Rules and rates can change; Crowded campaigns; host gives no income figures; Copyright/permissions depend on campaign terms

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Start a clipping campaign** (acquisition)
1. Find one active clipping campaign on Whop or Viral
2. Watch at least three winning clips in the same niche before opening an editor
3. Post the first clip within 48 hours
> "Post your first clip within 48 hours because speed is going to beat perfection's butt here every single time" — Host (~14:05)

**30-60 day proof-of-concept deadline** (mindset)
1. Pick one hustle
2. Set a minimum 30-day (preferably 60-day) deadline and write it down visibly
3. Do not switch ideas until the deadline passes

**Five-factor rubric to evaluate hustles** (operations)
1. Score each idea 1-5 on startup cost, skill barrier, weekly hours, time to first credible sale, and risk
2. Sum the scores (max 25); lower is better
3. Cross off ideas that don't fit your weekly hours

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Judge side hustles by the floor (time, cost and effort to first paid dollar), not the income ceiling.
- *customer_acquisition*: Plugging into existing audiences and payment systems (platforms, marketplaces) shortens time to first revenue.
- *customer_acquisition*: Selling to businesses with existing budgets and obvious pain points is faster than selling to anonymous consumers.
- *offer_design*: In AI service businesses, outreach and sales skill matter more than tool skill.
- *mindset_execution*: Commit to one idea with a fixed 30-60 day deadline to avoid shiny-object syndrome.
- *niche_selection*: Distribution, not production, is the bottleneck in AI-generated product businesses.
- *offer_design*: Sharpen the offer beyond 'I use ChatGPT' to avoid commoditised competition.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Many make-money-online videos hype ceilings but ignore time to first dollar; micro SaaS is a rough first hustle.
- Hopping between hustles every two or three weeks is what kills people.
- Avoid pricing wars in AI freelancing.

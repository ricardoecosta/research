## Tactics and playbook steps

**Prompt for strategies and angles** (product)
1. Ask for angles, tactics, and strategy instead of a broad request for facts.
2. Specify the type of opportunity, audience, or outcome you want the model to explore.
3. Use the response as a starting point and follow up on promising directions.
> "don't just ask it for facts ask it for angles tactics leverage instead of saying things like What are some good business ideas?" — Chris Koerner (~0:06)

**Give the model reference material** (operations)
1. State the audience and desired reading level or style.
2. Provide examples, images, video, or text that show what you want.
3. Ask the model what makes a set of examples effective, then reuse those findings in a project.
> "feed it as much reference data as you can whether that's an image a video or just text" — Chris Koerner (~1:07)

**Turn prompts into reusable workflows** (operations)
1. Write a prompt that can produce the same kind of result repeatedly.
2. Save it in a custom GPT or project.
3. Upload strong examples and ask the model to identify the qualities to reproduce.
> "you're not asking for answers you're asking for systems because the quality of your questions is the ceiling of your output" — Chris Koerner (~2:29)

**Test content angles with audience polls** (validation_testing)
1. Ask ChatGPT for eight marketing angles.
2. Run polls with four options at a time, asking which YouTube title people would click.
3. Let each poll run for about 30 minutes, then take one or two winners into a final bracket-style poll.
4. Use the winning angle to write the title, thumbnail, newsletter, or post.
> "i will take four of the eight marketing angles post them as a poll and say Which YouTube title would you click on?" — Chris Koerner (~3:23)

**Use customer reviews as research and content** (customer_acquisition)
1. Collect customer reviews for a business or niche.
2. Use the review language to identify customer problems and search terms.
3. Turn suitable review themes into keyword-focused content or a specific service offer.
4. Check the facts and obtain any needed permissions before publishing customer material.
> "you could just go to Google Maps you can just start searching around for businesses you could find any old business you can copy and paste all their reviews and then plug that into your chat as a source of reference" — Chris Koerner (~9:25)

**Compare local markets before choosing a business** (validation_testing)
1. Identify industries with poor reviews in a local area.
2. Choose comparable cities and compare business density and average review ratings.
3. Treat differences as leads for further validation, not proof that a new business will succeed.
> "there's two very very relevant data points that tell me I need to open a plumbing business in Houston because it's half as saturated" — Chris Koerner (~12:12)

**Refine answers across models** (product)
1. Ask the same question in several language models.
2. Compare the responses and choose the one with the most useful answer.
3. Show another model that response, explain what works or is missing, and ask it to improve.
> "take the same prompt the same question ask it to all four but instead of inserting that little sentence about jealousy don't even reference the other LLM just reference what the other LLM is saying and then ask it to do better" — Chris Koerner (~14:29)

## Lessons

- *automation_ai*: Ask AI for angles, strategies, and tactics when you need useful direction, rather than only requesting facts.
- *automation_ai*: Give AI examples and context so it can match the audience, style, and result you want.
- *operations*: Save prompts that work as reusable workflows, and improve them with examples of strong outputs.
- *automation_ai*: Treat the first AI response as a draft and refine it through follow-up questions and testing.
- *validation_testing*: Test marketing angles with real audience feedback before investing in a title or campaign.
- *offer_design*: Use simple language and build curiosity by promising a payoff, then delivering it at the end.
- *customer_acquisition*: Use customer reviews as evidence of what customers care about and what they search for.
- *niche_selection*: Start business brainstorming from your available skills, assets, time, and dislikes, then ask for more ideas like the best matches.
- *automation_ai*: Compare responses from multiple models and challenge them to improve the strongest answer.
- *operations*: Keep related instructions and files in projects to make repeated work more consistent.

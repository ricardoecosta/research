### I1. White-label AI video production for video and marketing agencies

- **Problem:** Agencies pay crews for low-margin shoots and risk losing clients to AI tools; most are not using AI yet
- **Offer:** Demo videos made for the agency's existing clients, then ongoing AI-generated ads, B-roll and UGC content delivered white-label
- **Business model:** Per-video fees or a monthly white-label retainer
- **Pricing:** per video or monthly retainer. agency retainer, low end USD 1000 per month; agency retainer, high end USD 4000 per month; Reddit creator's quoted commercial, low USD 10000 per job; Reddit creator's quoted commercial, high USD 15000 per job Agencies' clients reportedly pay $5-10k per video.

- **Unit economics (claims):** One agency client could be $1-4k per month; Veo costs $250/month (half off for 3 months); Reddit creator: $500 in credits and half a day vs $500k commercials

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Opt-in DM before pitching** (acquisition)
1. Find an agency's client from their portfolio
2. Make a Veo video for that client
3. DM the owner asking if you can send it
4. Send a Google Drive link once they agree
5. Follow up persistently. UK PECR: use company email for Ltd agencies, not unsolicited personal DMs.
> "Hey I know you have insert brand name as a client I just made a video for them Do you mind if I send it to you to see what you think" — Chris Koerner (~11:58)

**Weekend demo portfolio** (product)
1. Pick three niches (fitness, food, fashion/lifestyle)
2. Write very specific cinematic prompts
3. Render 5-10 second clips with music
4. Upload unbranded to Google Drive or Vimeo
> "Make each one of these in your portfolio about 5 to 10 seconds Use background music Polish it up Upload it to Google Drive or Vimeo with no branding" — Chris Koerner (~11:23)

**Reverse-engineer prompts from the Sora Explore page** (product)
1. Go to sora.com Explore, filter by top month and video
2. Copy the prompts behind the best videos
3. Paste a dozen into ChatGPT and ask for the first principles that make them work
> "Don't look at the videos look at the prompts that generated them and there is so much you can learn there" — Chris Koerner (~20:44)

**Flat monthly pricing over per-unit** (pricing)
1. Package a fixed number of videos per month at one price
2. Productize: one offer, one deliverable, one price
3. Sell via a landing page and Stripe link
> "You could say $50 a video and that is less enticing than $500 per month for 10 videos I promise you people just want to know what the bill is" — Chris Koerner (~13:12)

**$250 start plan** (acquisition)
1. Buy Veo
2. Make 3 ad-quality demos
3. Cold DM 30 (15 agencies, 15 brands)
4. Offer a $100 test video with 48-hour turnaround
5. Reinvest in the next 30 DMs by niche
6. Package into a $500/month offer after 2-3 paid videos. Check PECR before cold DMs to UK individuals and sole traders.
> "follow up with anyone who replies and offer a test video for 100 bucks No meetings no contracts" — Chris Koerner (~21:51)

**Anchor the upsell against a pricier alternative** (sales)
1. After the first paid video, ask to handle the rest of their content
2. Offer a bundled package
3. Compare the price with a social media manager costing 2-4x
> "And your pitch you're contrasting this against a social media manager that might cost two to four times that" — Chris Koerner (~23:54)

**Niche and reuse** (operations)
1. Serve one vertical at a time
2. Reuse prompt structures and the landing page across clients in that vertical
3. Copy the landing page to the next niche
> "You can literally copy and paste the landing page especially if you use something like card or web flow" — Chris Koerner (~15:17)

## Lessons

- *offer_design*: The AI tool isn't the product; the packaged offer is.
- *customer_acquisition*: Selling through agencies lets you piggyback on their client base while they handle client management.
- *customer_acquisition*: Visual products need a demo; a text pitch alone won't land.
- *customer_acquisition*: Ask permission to send the sample; an agreed opt-in makes the prospect feel obliged to watch.
- *pricing*: Flat, predictable monthly pricing beats per-unit pricing.
- *niche_selection*: Specialising in one niche lets you reuse content and prompts, halving the work.
- *validation_testing*: Land one paid job quickly to get a case study, then scale.
- *scaling*: Use the first sale as a foot in the door to upsell a full content retainer.
- *mindset_execution*: Document everything while learning; it becomes course material.
- *niche_selection*: Many traditional agencies aren't using AI yet; Twitter is a bubble.

## Warnings and mistakes

- The early-mover edge will erode within 6-12 months as competition arrives.
- Don't over-explain the tech to agencies; just show results.
- Expect many rejections; it's a numbers game.
- UK PECR (extractor note): the cold DM and text tactics aimed at individuals, sole-trader trades and agency owners' personal Instagram are unsolicited electronic marketing without consent. Use company email for limited companies, or in-person or phone outreach screened against TPS/CTPS.
- AI 'UGC' that looks like real customers may be misleading advertising (ASA/CMA); disclose AI-generated content (extractor note).

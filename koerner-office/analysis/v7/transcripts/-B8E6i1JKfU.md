## Tactics and playbook steps

**Price machine time and waste** (pricing)
1. Include machine running time in every quote
2. Add 10-15% waste allowance upfront
3. Rule: price the machine's time, not just materials
> "Price the machine's time, not just the materials" — Narrator (~11:53)

**Quote labels as monthly reorder** (pricing)
1. Target 3-4 local businesses that label products
2. Quote labels as a monthly line item, not a one-off job

**Index land leases to published averages** (legal)
1. Pull county parcel record
2. Get published county cash rent average from extension service
3. Write a one-year auto-renewing lease with rent review tied to the average
4. Stack grazing + hunting via master lease with carve-outs
> "No lease outlives its own renewal date" — Narrator (~30:01)

**Aggregate royalty contract terms** (legal)
1. Price by verified scale-house weight ticket
2. Index royalty to PPI for construction sand and gravel
3. Add reclamation clause and minimum annual guarantee

**Three filters for a spare-room business** (product)
1. Does bench, materials and packing fit with the door closing?
2. Is there a repeat buyer within driving distance? Name them before buying the machine
3. Does saved rent pay back the machine (e.g. $600/month rent vs $800 machine = ~6 weeks)?

**Land audit in 20 minutes** (product)
1. Open county parcel viewer
2. Record acres, road frontage, tree cover, distance to substation
3. Match to the seven lease types

## Lessons

- *customer_acquisition*: One-off sales are the sample; repeat local business buyers are the income.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Saved rent is the margin: a home business keeps what a shop pays its landlord.
- *niche_selection*: Low entry costs or modest equipment hurdles act as moats because serious people ignore them.
- *niche_selection*: Shrink the market: you need 40 buyers out of thousands, or 3-4 local accounts, not millions.
- *offer_design*: Zero-build leases: others bring equipment, you bring land and a signature.
- *legal_risk*: Permits and bonds that are hard to get are the strongest moats.
- *legal_risk*: Every lease needs a renewal date and an escalator.
- *offer_design*: Shared costs are the product: sell the spread between solo and shared mailing cost.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Underpricing a big order on materials only left Harry with almost no profit after a misaligned batch.
- A handshake grazing deal stayed at $10/acre while the county average rose; underpaid by a third, unrecoverable.
- Strangers on land: require club liability insurance naming you as additional insured.
- Flat 30-year solar leases shrink in real terms without an escalator.
- Chasing listing count instead of repeat buyers is a common mistake.
- Truck yards need commercial/industrial zoning or a conditional use permit.

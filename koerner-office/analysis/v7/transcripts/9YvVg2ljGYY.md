## Tactics and playbook steps

**Value and route a liquidation pallet** (operations)
1. Unbox every item and identify brand/model
2. Look up current price on Amazon, brand site or Target
3. Tally total retail value
4. Discount to ~60% to estimate realisable revenue
5. Sell bulky items locally (Facebook Marketplace, garage sales)

## Lessons

- *finance_unit_economics*: Value a pallet at a heavy discount (~60% of retail) after fees and markdowns.
- *operations*: Pallet flipping is labour-intensive and inefficient; physical handling dominates.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Single high-value items (e.g. a $499 litter box) can cover a pallet's cost.
- *validation_testing*: Results across two pallets differ widely; one data point is not a business.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Pallets vary hugely; the first was full of cheap or broken items.
- Heavy bulky items are hard to sell online and dangerous to handle.
- Retail-value tallies overstate what you will actually earn.

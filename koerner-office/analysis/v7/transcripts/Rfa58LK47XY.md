## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: A local sale can turn mixed surplus inventory into sales quickly, but the video does not establish whether proceeds exceed the acquisition cost.
- *operations*: Prepare prices and tags before opening so shoppers can browse a mixed assortment.
- *customer_acquisition*: Plan for early arrivals; shoppers may begin browsing before the advertised start time.
- *customer_acquisition*: Listing an attractive item online before the event can secure a buyer and create a reason for them to visit.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Separate proceeds from personal goods and business inventory when measuring a resale experiment.
- *automation_ai*: A garage sale depends on human setup, selling, and handover, so it is a poor fit for a founder seeking near-total agent operation.
- *operations*: Unfinished inventory needs a follow-on sales channel; the host considered continuing with marketplace listings after the event.

## Warnings and mistakes

- The reported $2,210 gross total included $485 from personal items; the host attributed $1,725 to pallet goods. Pallet purchase cost was not stated, so profit is unknown.
- A garage sale takes time to prepare, run, and take down, and the host said it was a lot of work.
- The host had substantial unsold inventory after the event, so the two-day sale did not clear the stock.
- The host offered toothpaste that had expired and told buyers about the expiry. Selling expired goods can create product-safety and legal risks.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Use the tool's own suggested prompts** (product)
1. Before prompting a new AI tool, browse the prompts it suggests
2. Learn its effects library (hover, animation)
3. Reuse those phrasings to get better output
> "anytime I use a new AI tool, I want to see what prompts it prompts me to use" — Chris Koerner (~0:58)

**Problem discovery with owners** (sales)
1. Pick an industry you understand (worked in or around it)
2. Ask general questions about their business
3. Dig in with what, why, how, when, who questions (who takes the lead? where does it go?)
4. Listen for hidden problems (e.g. staff absence losing leads)
5. Build several site versions quickly with prompts to solve them
> "Don't make the mistake of assuming that their problems are top of mind because they're usually not" — Chris Koerner (~11:29)

**Value-based pricing for fast delivery** (pricing)
1. Benchmark against agencies charging ~$3,000
2. Don't discount because AI makes you faster
3. Sell speed of delivery as a premium

**Add small design touches** (product)
1. Prompt for hover effects and animations
2. Check mobile responsiveness
3. Add lead-capture forms and test them end to end
> "I like to call these the moments between the moments" — Chris Koerner (~4:04)

## Lessons

- *pricing*: Charge for value created, not time spent.
- *niche_selection*: Pick the industry you understand best; that's your unfair advantage.
- *niche_selection*: Try several industries early rather than committing to one.
- *validation_testing*: If one business has a problem, others in the same industry probably do too.
- *automation_ai*: AI makes iteration cheap: four prompts give four alternative designs.
- *validation_testing*: Ship an MVP to get out into the world before polishing.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Don't assume business owners know their problems; you must dig.
- Asking an AI for fake reviews produces visible fake-review labels; placeholder reviews must be replaced (and fake reviews are illegal under UK consumer law, DMCC Act 2024).
- Wegic works best on desktop.

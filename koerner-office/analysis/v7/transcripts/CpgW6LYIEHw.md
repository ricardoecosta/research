## Tactics and playbook steps

**Automate invoice capture** (operations)
1. Connect Gmail to the agent
2. When an invoice email arrives, scan it
3. Log date, amount and attachment in a Google Sheet
4. Send sheet to accountant at tax time

**Email-to-quotation workflow** (sales)
1. Agent detects quote request email
2. Asks permission
3. Pulls latest prices and reseller discount from Google Sheet
4. Builds PDF quote in QuickBooks
5. Drafts reply with PDF for human to send

**Ad performance dashboard and creative refresh** (acquisition)
1. Pull data from Meta, Google, X
2. Track views, CPA, CTR per ad
3. Cut underperformers
4. Generate new images/videos to test

**Find a business from your own behaviour** (product)
1. Let an AI observe what you consume
2. Ask it what you spend time on
3. Mine comments on niche videos for pain points

**Triage DMs with an agent** (operations)
1. Monitor DMs and log to a Google Sheet
2. Search for high-value ones (podcast invites, success stories)

**Run many cheap tests** (product)
1. Build multiple simple storefronts
2. Share with friends and groups
3. Keep the one that gets positive feedback

## Lessons

- *automation_ai*: AI agents mostly let you do more work, not less: an 8-hour day yields 16-30 hours of output.
- *niche_selection*: 'What business should I start' requires deep personal context; your consumption habits reveal niches.
- *validation_testing*: Pain points for a niche are often already in comments on niche videos.
- *validation_testing*: The cost of building a storefront is near zero; run many trials until one clicks.
- *niche_selection*: Technology is unevenly distributed: localise existing tools for markets not yet exposed.
- *pricing*: Subscription fatigue is real; users dislike many $20 monthly bills.
- *automation_ai*: Agents should ask permission before irreversible actions.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Small on-device models are still far less capable than frontier models (GPT-3.5/4 level).
- Irreversible agent actions (deleting files, sending messages) should require user permission.
- Kickstarter price will rise; early-bird urgency is promotional.

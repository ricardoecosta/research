## Lessons

- *legal_risk*: In health products, describe what the product can do today precisely; listeners may interpret a claim about future capability as a claim that it works now.
- *legal_risk*: A founder's mission can become a justification for deception if they treat the intended future benefit as permission to misrepresent current results.
- *validation_testing*: Do not confuse confidence or persuasive presence with evidence that a product works or that a speaker is being truthful.
- *validation_testing*: Keep product claims tied to validated performance; the hosts describe a large gap between the tests claimed and those actually run on Theranos machines.
- *operations*: High-stakes products need clear accountability between founders, partners, and operators; the hosts discuss how power dynamics can affect oversight.
- *customer_acquisition*: Direct questions followed by silence can surface revealing answers in interviews.
- *other*: A close view of people can reveal behavior without resolving what they believe or what happened; seek independent evidence before drawing conclusions.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Pitch partners with finished work instead of asking permission** (acquisition)
1. Build a body of work/results first
2. Approach the target partner (e.g. Beehiiv) showing what you already did
3. Frame it as 'we need to work together'
4. Result: largest partnership, one-year deal
> "Do not wait for anybody's permission. It's not coming." — Millie Tumedy (~8:53)

**Creative mornings, meetings afternoons** (operations)
1. Walk and meditate before going online
2. Spend mornings on content creation before Slack/email
3. Afternoons for meetings, podcasts, partnerships
4. Hard stop ~3pm; off means off
> "So my mornings are for creations. Thing that requires my creative juices to be flowing." — Millie Tumedy (~4:16)

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Fall in love with a big, unsolvable problem rather than an idea; it gives long-term drive.
- *mindset_execution*: Design the business around the life you want; a few hundred thousand a year with no investors can be the goal.
- *customer_acquisition*: Don't wait for permission from partners; show completed work.
- *mindset_execution*: Calm, grounded mornings improve decisions on calls and partnerships.
- *customer_acquisition*: Being more weird and honest differentiates in a bland internet.
- *offer_design*: A niche identity (generalists) can support multiple revenue lines: newsletter, community, job board, events.
- *scaling*: Scale choice matters: thousands of customers bring support load that changes lifestyle.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Picking a business you think you 'should' do, without asking why it needs to exist, leads astray.
- Chasing the arbitrary million-dollar narrative can cost health and family.

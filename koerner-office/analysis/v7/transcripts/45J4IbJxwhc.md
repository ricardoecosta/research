## Tactics and playbook steps

**Character reference sheet workflow** (product)
1. Generate a character in Higgsfield (choose type, build, hair, style, features) or upload a photo
2. Download it and ask ChatGPT for a full-body front, back, side and face reference sheet, referencing an ultra-realism prompt
3. Use the sheet as the reference image for every video or image so the character stays consistent
> "give me a full-body image front, back, side, and face" — George McConnell (~15:44)

**Place character in a scene for trend video** (product)
1. Create a scene image of the character in a location (e.g. Eiffel Tower, laundromat) using ChatGPT
2. Add the reference photo and scene image in Seedance 2.5 inside Higgsfield
3. Write a prompt for the trend action (shadow boxing, northern soul dance)
4. Set 13 seconds, 9:16, 1080p, high bitrate and generate

**Own-rights motion reference** (legal)
1. Pick a viral dance on TikTok as inspiration only
2. Have a person film themselves recreating it, or have AI create a reference video
3. Upload that as the reference video with the character image and generate with no prompt
> "you need to have rights to that video" — George McConnell (~9:28)

**Run a short-term trend account and a long-term account in parallel** (acquisition)
1. Make weird trend characters to capture current virality
2. Keep a realistic character for sustained growth
3. Mix images (e.g. character with celebrities) and videos

## Lessons

- *scaling*: Trend-riding gives fast reach, but pair it with an evergreen asset because trends decay.
- *operations*: Consistent characters need a multi-angle reference sheet to keep generations consistent.
- *legal_risk*: Own the rights to source material; filming your own reference avoids copyright problems.
- *niche_selection*: Realistic, relatable personas are likely to outlast bizarre ones.
- *validation_testing*: Free tool tiers let you test AI content ideas with near-zero cost before committing.
- *finance_unit_economics*: The video never explains how an AI influencer earns money; a monetisation plan (sponsorships, products, affiliates) must be worked out separately.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Do not upload other people's TikTok or Instagram videos as reference footage; you need rights to reference videos or risk getting shut down.
- Viral AI character trends die quickly, and late meme coin buyers lost money.
- AI generation may alter the background and details of the reference scene, and weird male character output looked less real.

## Summary

Chris Koerner interviews Ryan Dozer, a solo AI marketer who runs a six-figure SEO/content agency. Dozer explains Claude skills (markdown SOPs for AI agents) and how he packaged about 20-25 of his own into a $99 'Claude Code skill stack' sold from his own site via Stripe, claiming over $3,000 in 30-45 days. He demonstrates an SEO blog-post-writer skill that turns YouTube videos into ranking articles, a LinkedIn scraper skill using Apify, and a vibe-coded marketing OS. Key takeaway: turn the workflows and expertise you already use daily into skills and sell them as digital products, but the numbers are self-reported and the sales path leans on his existing audience.

### I1. Claude Code skill stack digital product

- **Customer:** Marketers and power users of Claude Code, Codex or other AI agents who want ready-made skills
- **Problem:** Users must build their own prompts and SOPs for AI agents and copy-paste between GPTs and projects, which is tedious
- **Offer:** Bundle of about 20-25 markdown skill files (SEO blog post writer, YouTube thumbnail designer, anti-slop skill, web designer etc.) plus Claude.md tips, delivered as downloads
- **Business model:** One-off digital product sales via own website and Stripe, promoted by soft-sell CTAs in content and email
- **Pricing:** One-off bundle price. Claude Code skill stack USD 99 one-off One sale came from a $1,000 one-hour consulting upsell, so not all revenue was $99 sales.
- **Acquisition:** seo_organic_search: Blog posts and a new SEO test site gaining traffic from Google and LLMs, with a floating popup and CTAs in blog posts; existing_audience: CTAs in email newsletters and drip sequences; YouTube channel and community (AI Marketing Insiders); youtube: Videos are repurposed into SEO blog posts that link back to the product
- **Delivery:** Customer gets a page with download links to markdown files, then repurposes them with their own context. Fully digital, no human delivery per sale.
- **Unit economics (claims):** Over $3,000 in 30-45 days, described as passive; Priced at $99 with 20-25 skills; Only Stripe fees (about 3%) as cost, plus his time
- **Tools:** Claude Code, Claude skill creator skill, Stripe, Visual Studio Code, Opus 4.7, GPT Image 2
- **Startup cost:** Not stated beyond existing Claude subscription and time; landing page built in about 15-20 minutes with Claude Code
- **Time to first revenue:** Launched about 30-45 days before recording and already over $3,000
- **UK applicability [E]:** 5/5 — Digital product sold via Stripe works the same in the UK; VAT and digital-services rules apply.
- **Risks:** Revenue is self-reported and partly a $1,000 consulting upsell; Skills are easily copied or replaced by Anthropic's free skills; Sales depend on an existing audience; Model and platform changes can make skills obsolete; Low price point needs sustained traffic
- **Automation [E]:** acquisition 3/5 (SEO blog posts, email drips and popups can be agent-produced, but the traffic relies on the founder's existing audience and authority.); onboarding 5/5 (Purchase leads straight to a download page.); delivery 5/5 (Static markdown files delivered digitally.); billing 5/5 (Stripe payment link.); support 3/5 (Low volume; an FAQ and an agent for emails, with occasional human help. ## Tactics and playbook steps

**Convert a Claude project into a skill** (product)
1. Install Anthropic's free skill creator skill from their GitHub
2. Open the project and voice-dump an instruction to repurpose the project into a skill markdown file, keeping instructions, memory and recent chat history
3. Refine the output, then download the markdown file
4. Use it in the web app or move it to another AI agent
> "use the skill creator skill to transform or repurpose this project into a into a skill markdown file" — Chris Koerner (~12:30)

**Granular one-skill-per-task design** (product)
1. Create one skill per task (SEO blog writer, newsletter writer, sales email writer)
2. Avoid one giant broad skill
3. Tell the skill to update itself when you correct mistakes
> "I have one clawed skill per marketing task." — Ryan Dozer (~4:20)

**Build the sales page with your own skills** (acquisition)
1. Finish the digital product
2. Give it to Claude Code with a web designer skill that knows branding, tone and logo
3. Supply the Stripe payment link
4. Add social proof and FAQs; page done in about 15-20 minutes
5. Promote with a floating popup, blog CTAs and email/drip CTAs, soft sell
> "in about 15 20 minutes this Claude Code skill stack was complete." — Ryan Dozer (~16:46)

**Dogfooding content flywheel** (acquisition)
1. Use your skills to produce SEO content
2. Ship content to a site that sells the same skill stack
3. Add soft CTAs in posts and emails

**Reduce AI hallucination with a check line** (operations)
1. End skill prompts with an instruction to triple check the skill instructions
2. Give the model keywords and exact link lists
> "Triple check the SEO blog post writer instructions when doing this task" — Ryan Dozer (~25:09)

**Use markdown or text instead of PDFs for AI context** (operations)
1. Convert transcripts and documents to markdown or .txt
2. Feed that to agents as context
> "they struggle with PDFs more than they do markdown" — Ryan Dozer (~20:21)

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Monetise workflows you already use daily; building for yourself first gives a validated product and better client work.
- *niche_selection*: Sell into smaller, less crowded marketplaces and ecosystems rather than the crowded main app stores.
- *automation_ai*: Portable, plain-text skills protect against platform lock-in because they transfer across AI agents.
- *niche_selection*: Expertise in any niche can become a product, not just marketing.
- *customer_acquisition*: Soft-sell CTAs across content, email and drip sequences can sell a low-priced digital product without hard pushing.
- *operations*: Use AI to build your landing page and payment flow quickly, cutting launch time to minutes.
- *pricing*: Skills turn into consulting upsells; a product can open high-value service conversations.
- *mindset_execution*: Move from copy-paste chat workflows to agentic tools and learn them through daily tasks.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Do not plug and play free skills; add your own context, instructions and style guide or outputs will not be relevant.
- Google heavily penalises mass-generated AI slop and SEO content factories.
- AI can hallucinate, so add verification instructions.

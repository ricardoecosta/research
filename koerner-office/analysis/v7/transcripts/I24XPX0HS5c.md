## Summary

Chris Koerner demos OpenAI's new ChatGPT Agent mode, running six agents at once on business tasks: finding Nashville plumbers with websites, hyper-personalised cold emails to Austin dentists, competitor research for his Texas Snacks store, Google Trends ranking of business ideas, a calendar meeting-briefing task, and Amazon-review research that became a pitch deck. He pitches selling websites or SEO to businesses that already have websites, and selling AI-driven research to clients. He rates each task out of 10 and notes friction: logins, glitches when he navigated away from the tab, and slow runs. Takeaway: agents save hours of research and drafting, but need specific prompts, reference data and some supervision.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Target businesses that already have websites** (acquisition)
1. Pick a local trade and city
2. Have the agent find businesses that already have websites and list owner contact info
3. Skip judging the site quality; having a site signals they value it
4. Pitch redesign or improvement
> "if you go give this same offer to business owners that don't have a website, they could have one by now." — Chris Koerner (~4:41)

**Give the agent reference data and name sources** (operations)
1. Provide examples (e.g. four sample ideas) so output matches your taste
2. Name specific sources such as Manta, Reddit, Facebook, X and niche blogs
3. Say 'move fast' to speed it up
4. Ask for niche, specific case studies to avoid generic articles
> "Anytime you ask it to do a task or to return something interesting for you, give it reference data." — Chris Koerner (~6:26)

**Mine Amazon reviews for product gaps** (product)
1. Ask the agent to research reviews for similar products
2. Extract common complaints and rank by proportion with a pie chart
3. Create a feature list addressing the top pain points
4. Turn findings into a pitch deck
> "I don't want to fix problems that are anecdotal and don't affect a large percentage of people." — Chris Koerner (~16:56)

**Run multiple agents in parallel and minimise logins** (operations)
1. Open several agent tabs for independent tasks
2. Avoid tasks needing account logins where possible
3. Do not navigate away from tabs inside logged-in accounts
4. Ask for CSV export if Google Sheets glitches
> "The less you ask it to log in for now, the better." — Chris Koerner (~33:58)

**Case-study-to-launch-plan prompt** (validation)
1. Have the agent rank low-cost ideas using Google Trends
2. Pick one and request niche case studies with startup costs and early sales
3. Ask for 40 stories, then a 30-day launch plan
> "Come up with a dayby-day launch plan over the next 30 days." — Chris Koerner (~24:09)

## Lessons

- *automation_ai*: Specific prompts with named sources and reference examples beat generic prompts.
- *customer_acquisition*: The value of AI outreach is the research before the email, not the sending.
- *niche_selection*: Sell to people who already value the thing (those with a website, clean driveways), not those who plainly need it but don't care.
- *validation_testing*: Use research agents to validate demand cheaply by mining reviews, Reddit and YouTube before building a product.
- *validation_testing*: Once you have proprietary data from an agent, keep questioning it to find which problems are widespread.
- *mindset_execution*: Speed of adoption matters: people who automate workflows early gain a cost advantage over those treating AI as a search engine.
- *legal_risk*: Platform risk: features absorbed by OpenAI can erase wrapper businesses.
- *operations*: Agent tools remain slow and glitchy for multi-login tasks, so plan for human supervision.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Agent glitched in Gmail and Google Sheets when the user switched tabs; logins are friction and expose sensitive data.
- Agent put the whole email body in the subject line and wrote spammy subject lines, so review drafts before sending.
- Generic prompts produce generic ideas (pet sitting, dog walking); give niche reference examples.
- OpenAI may make Chrome extensions and apps built on its APIs irrelevant.

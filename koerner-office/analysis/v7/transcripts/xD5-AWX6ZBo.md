## Lessons

- *mindset_execution*: Treat uncomfortable feedback as useful information when it comes from someone who wants to help and has no obvious self-serving motive.
- *mindset_execution*: Notice when confidence is based on a vague belief that you are exceptional; make your strengths more specific so you rely less on other people's approval.
- *validation_testing*: Planning can feel like hard work while avoiding the real test: finding out whether customers want the offer.
- *validation_testing*: For a physical business, get to a location, open, serve customers, and learn from their response instead of polishing a plan indefinitely.
- *mindset_execution*: Act on advice quickly and return with what you did and learned; repeated follow-through helped the founders earn a mentor's respect.
- *mindset_execution*: A person can choose a new identity and reinforce it through repeated actions, even when it feels unfamiliar at first.
- *mindset_execution*: After an embarrassing setback, ask what you gained from it and use the answer to grow.

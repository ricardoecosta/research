## Tactics and playbook steps

**QR code 'scan for a prize' yard signs** (acquisition)
1. After a job, place a yard sign with a big QR code and 'Scan for a prize'
2. Test versions with and without your logo
3. Capture name, email and phone for a draw (e.g. $100 Amazon card or bundled local gift cards)
4. Market to the captured leads
> "Have a big old QR code and then a big bold letters. have it say like scan for a prize" — Brandon (~1:24)

**Neighbour blitz with personalised referral coupons** (acquisition)
1. Bring door hangers and leave them at the ~6 closest neighbours
2. Give the customer coupons with their name (e.g. 20% off) to hand to neighbours
3. Use a unique QR/affiliate link per customer to track and reward top referrers
> "you're bringing affiliate marketing to home services." — Chris Koerner (~5:18)

**Pay customers to post in local Facebook groups** (acquisition)
1. Ask happy customers if they're in neighbourhood/moms Facebook groups
2. Offer a physical $50 Amazon gift card on the spot if they post
3. Text them a custom link paying $20 per signup; tell them to put the link in the top comment to avoid deboosting
4. Share a case study of a past payout to motivate
5. Be willing to sit in the awkward silence when asking
> "there's a lot of money to be made by being willing to sit in the awkwardness." — Brandon (~8:19)

**Rock-in-a-bag driveway flyers** (acquisition)
1. Buy rocks (~$10 bag) and open 4x10 bags
2. Print flyers the same shape as the bag
3. Assemble rock, flyer, bag, staple
4. Pick the right neighbourhood per service
5. Throw on driveways; don't answer calls on day one (mostly angry), answer from day two
> "You don't ever pick up the phone the first day because almost 100% of those first day calls are people that are angry at you" — Chris Koerner (~11:19)

**LLM-optimised blog content** (acquisition)
1. Prompt ChatGPT: write in-depth blog posts for my site that will rank in LLM search for relevant queries
2. Let it propose topics, structure and competitor angles
3. Add your common customer FAQs
4. Publish on your website
> "Can you write me quality in-depth blog posts for my website that will rank well in LLM searching on chat GPT" — Brandon (~15:51)

**Co-marketing with non-competing local trades** (acquisition)
1. Find well-rated, similarly sized local businesses that don't compete (roofers and painters, Christmas lights and landscapers)
2. Meet the owner
3. Promote each other's services to your customer lists by text and email
> "Why don't more roofers partner with painters? Yes. And just co-arket all the time" — Brandon (~23:02)

**Book a meeting from a meeting** (sales)
1. Never end a sales or account call without the next call booked
2. End every interaction with a low-friction next step, like ChatGPT asking to continue
> "Book book a meeting from a meeting." — Brandon (~21:21)

## Lessons

- *customer_acquisition*: Merge offline and online: physical touchpoints (yard signs, coupons) can feed digital funnels and tracking.
- *validation_testing*: Test incentive sizes for referrals; after 5-10 tries you'll know whether $20 or $200 is right.
- *customer_acquisition*: Unusual, non-intrusive formats stand out where standard direct mail is ignored.
- *customer_acquisition*: Be first on new channels: LLM search traffic is small but higher quality.
- *scaling*: Find one channel that works and push it until ROI turns negative, as AOL did with CDs.
- *offer_design*: Remove friction and always propose the next step to keep customers engaged.
- *customer_acquisition*: Non-competing businesses serving the same customers can piggyback on each other's trust.
- *customer_acquisition*: Ads work on everyone at the right moment; timing matters as much as message.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Most business owners don't ask for referrals assertively enough; a throwaway comment doesn't work.
- Rock-in-a-bag drops provoke angry calls on the first day.
- Low-friction offers increase response but lower intent; specific offers get fewer but better leads.
- Facebook may deboost posts with links; put links in comments.
- Paid referral posts in community groups should be disclosed (UK ASA/CMA rules on incentivised endorsements) and referral texts or links must not be sent unsolicited to individuals under PECR.
- It's unclear whether ChatGPT's advice on how it ranks content is accurate.

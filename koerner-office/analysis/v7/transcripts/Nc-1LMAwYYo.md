## Tactics and playbook steps

**Rebuild the website for conversion, not looks** (acquisition)
1. Copy a proven home-service layout (Tommy Mello's) - 'rip off and duplicate'
2. Target local keywords and geo-tag
3. Use AI to build it
4. Expect leads to rise (3 to 30 a month here)
> "the keywords, um, the geo tagging, the the wireframe wasn't it wasn't set up to convert like the website was just set up to look nice" — Allan (~24:14)

**Diagnose sales before buying leads** (sales)
1. Schedule time to review VA sales call recordings
2. Train VAs to build rapport in discovery
3. Teach them to hold frame and keep calls short
4. Fire VAs who perform badly
> "because I thought I had a lead problem. No, we had a sales problem" — Allan (~13:18)

**Live video testimonials** (acquisition)
1. Collect live video testimonials from customers
2. Post them on the Google profile and website
3. Use testimonials from well-known local business owners for credibility
> "we like asking for like live testimonials" — Allan (~37:25)

**Focus on one market** (mindset)
1. Shut down or sell secondary locations
2. Pick the strongest city (Lawrence) and abandon others
3. Use a social-media blocking app and calendar time blocks
> "I just completely abandoned Witch, just started focusing on Lawrence" — Allan (~21:33)

**Vet cleaners** (hiring)
1. Test-clean candidates
2. Run background checks
3. Remove cleaners who show carelessness
> "which is why you got to test clean them background check them you know" — Johnny (~45:23)

## Lessons

- *mindset_execution*: Focus on one business and one market before adding more.
- *customer_acquisition*: Often the constraint is sales conversion, not lead volume; review calls.
- *mindset_execution*: Copy proven layouts and playbooks rather than inventing.
- *customer_acquisition*: Marketing should pre-sell so well that untrained staff can close.
- *mindset_execution*: Entrepreneurship is a strong path for people with criminal records who struggle to get hired.
- *customer_acquisition*: A remote service business can look bigger than it is; consistent presence makes recruits and customers assume scale.
- *customer_acquisition*: Conviction in your service and a satisfaction guarantee improve sales.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Running several businesses and markets at once divides results.
- Buying businesses brings transition chaos: tax ID delays, money on hold, expiring paperwork.
- Careless cleaners cause costly damage; cleaners are your product.
- Rogue staff may steal clients and harass the team.
- Pretty direct mail without an offer or copy wasted money.
- Being busy is not being productive.
- Hosts sell the Home Service Academy programme; guest is a client.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**City-by-item SEO page expansion** (acquisition)
1. Pick specific fixed-price items (TV, couch, washer)
2. Create unique pages per item per city and nearby suburbs
3. Write unique content to avoid doorway-page penalties; internal links and on-page SEO
4. Expand to a new city by adding pages there

**Recruit local subcontractors** (hiring)
1. Search Thumbtack, Craigslist and Google listings in the target area
2. Skip big brands; choose smaller operators with solid reputations
3. Call: we're getting jobs in your area, can we send them to you?
4. Line up a couple per area; pay near their normal rate

**Automate booking and dispatch** (operations)
1. Use Zenbooker for item selection, upfront pricing and payment
2. Auto-send jobs to subs in the area to accept
3. Subs mark complete with photo; owner pays them

**Start local, then expand** (acquisition)
1. Get a Google Business listing and website in your town
2. Post in local Facebook community pages
3. Grow via word of mouth and SEO into other towns

## Lessons

- *offer_design*: Customers care that the job is done at the price paid, not who does it.
- *customer_acquisition*: Hyper-specific item+city pages can rank where generalists don't.
- *automation_ai*: Fixed upfront pricing is the key enabler of remote, dispatch-based service businesses.
- *validation_testing*: A single out-of-area order can reveal a scalable model; test it instead of cancelling.
- *pricing*: Pay subcontractors near their normal rate and aim for a consistent margin per job.
- *niche_selection*: Local home services are beginner-friendly and more scalable than people think.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Duplicate city pages are treated as doorway pages and demoted by Google.
- Drivers not showing up and customer issues still happen.
- Only works for services that can be priced upfront without an on-site quote.
- Don't expect the same results; do your own research.

2. Ask an LLM to turn that material into a quiz with questions and recommendation logic.

## Lessons

- *customer_acquisition*: Offer a prospect something useful before asking for a sale; a tailored deck or research report can make the first contact more relevant.
- *automation_ai*: Use AI to synthesise existing information and produce a useful starting point; the speakers see it as less reliable at producing genuinely novel ideas.
- *operations*: When a task is vague, give the LLM context and examples of the output you want before asking it to generate a result.
- *automation_ai*: Reverse-engineer a prompt from examples you like, then test and refine it rather than expecting the first prompt to be perfect.
- *offer_design*: A quiz can make an abstract recommendation feel personal and can collect structured information as people engage.
- *niche_selection*: Choose a focused niche rather than trying to serve every job seeker or business with one general product.
- *validation_testing*: Questions about real past choices and preferences may reveal more than asking someone to rate themselves.
- *mindset_execution*: Build a small working version and learn from it; the speakers describe buying a domain and starting a prototype immediately.
- *operations*: Treat AI research outputs as material to verify and interpret, especially when charts show correlations or source data is incomplete.

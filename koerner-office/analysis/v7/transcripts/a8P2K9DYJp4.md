## Tactics and playbook steps

**Use a post-fix-sell workflow** (operations)
1. Source a free or unwanted appliance.
2. Post a listing before repair to see whether a buyer or renter responds.
3. Diagnose and repair the appliance if demand looks promising.
4. Retest it, clean it and complete the sale or rental.
> "The big secret about everything we did is we posted before we even fixed the thing. We didn't even know who it if it would work." — Chris Koerner (~15:04)

**Diagnose common dryer failures with a multimeter** (operations)
1. Check whether the dryer powers on.
2. Remove the back panel and test the thermal fuse for continuity.
3. If it spins but does not heat, test the heating element.
4. Inspect the belt and roller wheels, remove lint, replace failed parts and retest.
> "If it beeps, it's good, and I have no idea what's wrong. If it doesn't beep, we need a new one." — Kyler (~3:33)

**Choose price based on how quickly the unit should sell** (pricing)
1. Use the higher $175 to $200 range if willing to wait a week or two.
2. Use the $140 to $150 range for a more aggressive same-day sale.
3. Describe that the unit is clean, tested, working and heats properly.
> "If you want to sell it the same day, I would price it more aggressively in the 140 to 150 range. That's normally what I do cuz I I do high volume a little bit lower price." — Kyler (~11:30)

**List the same appliance for sale and rent** (customer_acquisition)
1. Create one Marketplace listing for a one-off sale.
2. Create a separate rental listing at a monthly price.
3. Use inbound responses to gauge demand for each option.
> "Thankfully, it did. Uh and we posted it twice in two different ways. 175 to sell and 45 to rent." — Chris Koerner (~15:12)

**Make delivery and basic assurance part of the rental offer** (offer_design)
1. State the monthly price clearly in the listing.
2. Offer free delivery and installation.
3. Describe maintenance and the 30-day guarantee.
4. Use the offer to reduce the time a renter spends without a working dryer.
> "And then I always include that we will warranty it for 30 days for a 30-day guarantee. And then the real trick is offer free delivery within 30 minutes." — Kyler (~11:56)

**Use delivery to recover more inventory** (operations)
1. Ask about the renter's old appliance during delivery.
2. Take the old dryer back when the customer offers it.
3. Inspect it as a potential repair, resale or rental unit.
> "And the other reason we do it is every time you go deliver a dryer to someone, 90% of the time they give you their old dryer back." — Kyler (~12:10)

**Use an online lead source to make appliance supply more consistent** (scaling)
1. Collect homeowner requests to get rid of working or broken appliances.
2. Share or sell those leads to local operators.
3. Let operators choose which appliances to collect and whether to flip, rent or use rent-to-own.
> "Finding dryers and washers on the side of the road isn't the most consistent way. So, we built a website called TakeMyAppliance that connects homeowners who want to get rid of their appliances, whether they're working or not working, with people like me and you who want to go and flip the appliances or rent out the appliances." — Kyler (~17:11)

**Use edited photos and clear Marketplace listings** (customer_acquisition)
1. Take photos of the appliance.
2. Use ChatGPT to clean up the appearance of listing photos.
3. State the actual condition, included cable and any changes the buyer can request.
4. Post the listing on Facebook Marketplace and relevant local groups.
> "All you're going to do is put in the dirty pics into ChatGPT and then ask it to clean it up a little bit." — Kyler (~10:21)

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Test demand before spending more time on a repair: listing early can reveal interest while the unit is still being fixed.
- *pricing*: Choose pricing based on the desired time to sale; a lower price may move inventory faster, while a higher price may require waiting.
- *offer_design*: A single asset can be offered through different money models: a quick sale or a recurring rental.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Rental revenue can be recurring, but the total value of a long rental takes time to collect and depends on the customer staying.
- *niche_selection*: Combining rentals with flipping gives the operator another way to earn when rental demand is lower.
- *scaling*: A specialized lead website can address an inconsistent supply problem that makes a local resale business harder to scale.
- *automation_ai*: For this kind of physical business, online listings can automate discovery, but repair, delivery and maintenance still depend on people.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Lint buildup can make the dryer overheat, work less well or clog the roller wheels; clean the unit and its components.
- The repair process involves electrical and heating components, but the video does not describe a full safety procedure or formal training.
- The demonstrated listing photo is edited to add a cable; listing images should not misrepresent the actual appliance or included parts.
- Delivery can require difficult physical handling; the demonstration unit had to be taken into a basement apartment.

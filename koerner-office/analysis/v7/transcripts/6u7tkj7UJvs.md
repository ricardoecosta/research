## Tactics and playbook steps

**Trend-picker content research** (acquisition)
1. Connect Higgsfield to Claude via custom connector (MCP)
2. Run the Trend Picker skill for your niche
3. Review top viral videos, hooks, hashtags and creators
4. Make your own version of the best-performing formats

**Clone a viral video format** (acquisition)
1. Find a viral TikTok in your niche
2. Paste its URL into Supercomputer
3. Ask for a version in your own style, changing little because it's proven
4. Add free-audit link in bio

**Free audit foot-in-door for AI agency** (sales)
1. Offer a low-cost or free AI audit
2. Convert to $1-5K/month retainer
3. Use generated flyer, PDF, website and TikToks as marketing

**Give the work away first** (sales)
1. Create sample work (listing package or creator content plan)
2. Deliver it free
3. Pitch ongoing paid work

## Lessons

- *customer_acquisition*: Distribution, not building, is the bottleneck, so tools that automate content and trend-finding address the hard part.
- *validation_testing*: Copy a proven viral format and make it your own rather than starting from zero.
- *offer_design*: Giving free sample work demonstrates effort and can convert to recurring paid work.
- *automation_ai*: Reading an AI tool's reasoning shows how it works and suggests better prompts.
- *pricing*: A lightweight audit as an entry offer leading to retainers is a common agency structure.
- *mindset_execution*: Set a time-boxed test (one month) before pivoting on a new content experiment.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Distribution (sales and marketing) is harder than ever even though software is easy to build.
- If the AI influencer isn't working after a month, pivot rather than persist.

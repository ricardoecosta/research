## Tactics and playbook steps

**Profit-first bucket split** (finance)
1. Open multiple sub-accounts (taxes, payroll, profit, opex)
2. On each payment, auto-split e.g. 30% taxes, 20% profit, 40% opex, 10% payroll
3. Run the business on what's left
> "you could send 30% to taxes, 20% to profit, 40% to operating expenses, and 10% to payroll" — Casper Capital narrator (~3:31)

**Open a business bank account** (finance)
1. Get an EIN from the IRS (free)
2. Bring formation papers, business address and operating agreement if partners
3. Keep personal and business money separate

## Lessons

- *legal_risk*: Separate business and personal finances for taxes, profit tracking and LLC liability protection.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Pick a bank by your cash, wire and balance profile; fees add up fast for cash-heavy or wire-heavy businesses.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Sub-account buckets automate profit-first discipline.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Idle balances can earn 1-3.5% APY in fintech accounts instead of nothing.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Fintech partner perks (processing credits, free software) can offset first-year costs.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Traditional big-bank accounts quietly charge service, deposit, wire, overdraft and ATM fees.
- Novo does not support direct cash deposits.
- Rates and features change often; check current offers (video uses affiliate links).

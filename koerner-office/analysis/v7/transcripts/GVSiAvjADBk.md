## Summary

Spencer Haws of Niche Pursuits lists the five types of websites he would build to reach $5,000 a month within 12 months: SaaS tools, e-commerce with your own product, free tool websites monetised by ads, paid membership communities, and newsletters grown with paid ads against known revenue per subscriber. He backs each with examples (his Link Whisper and Long Tail Pro, his pillow brand sold for $425k, youtubethumbnaildownloader.com, ZeroGPT, Creator Science Lab, MarketBeat). He also says to avoid search-dependent blogs, affiliate sites and social-traffic sites because of AI, Google and platform algorithm risk. Key takeaway: build websites with utility or owned audiences rather than search-dependent content.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Find a SaaS idea from your own pain** (product)
1. Notice a repetitive task that costs you hours (internal linking)
2. Search for existing tools; if none fit your platform (WordPress), build it
3. Use AI coding tools to ship an MVP
> "What is a pain point that people in your niche are having? What is something that people will actually pay for" — Spencer Haws (~1:27)

**Paid newsletter growth flywheel** (acquisition)
1. Pick a niche with sponsorship potential
2. Measure revenue per subscriber
3. Run paid ads on Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn/Google
4. Keep cost per subscriber below revenue per subscriber and scale spend
> "the goal is to optimize your ads so that it costs you less money to add a new subscriber than you make on an average subscriber" — Spencer Haws (~8:11)

**Own store first, marketplaces as gravy** (acquisition)
1. Launch your own product on Shopify
2. Add Amazon and other marketplaces as secondary channels
3. Find a profitable paid ad campaign (spend $1, make $2)

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Focus on websites with utility (software, tools) rather than pure content in the AI search era.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Know your cost per acquisition and revenue per customer; then scale as long as the gap is positive.
- *offer_design*: Owning your own product beats affiliate or dropshipping for durability.
- *customer_acquisition*: Treat Google, affiliate and social traffic as gravy, not the main source.
- *automation_ai*: AI coding tools lower the barrier to shipping a software MVP; the bottleneck is the idea.
- *offer_design*: Communities can be valuable because people crave human interaction as AI spreads.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Don't start a blog or content site reliant on Google or other organic search traffic if you want $5k/month in 12 months, unless you have an alternative traffic source or ad arbitrage.
- Affiliate sites are risky: programs can be taken away and Google dependence is fragile.
- Websites relying on social traffic (Pinterest, Facebook) fight platforms that keep users on-platform and face algorithm risk.
- Revenue figures for third-party tool sites are estimates, not verified.

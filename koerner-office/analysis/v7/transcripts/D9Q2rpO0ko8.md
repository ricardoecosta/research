## Tactics and playbook steps

**No-brainer performance offer** (sales)
1. Ask the owner if they have a customer list and roughly how many emails
2. Offer to email the whole list with a seasonal special for free
3. Take 10% of any revenue generated; nothing if zero
4. Alternative: charge $1,000 or 10%, whichever is less, to guarantee some payment
> "I charge a,000 bucks for this or 10% of any revenue I bring you or whichever is less" — Chris Koerner (~9:42)

**Build the reactivation workflow in GoHighLevel** (operations)
1. Automation > Create workflow > From template > search 'reactivation' > Recipe List Reactivation
2. Import client CSV via Contacts > Import, map fields, apply a tag
3. Set the tag as the workflow trigger
4. Edit the email/SMS offer with first-name personalisation
5. Wait for reply up to 1 hour, check if positive, auto-reply with booking link
6. Add follow-up steps and publish
7. Review delivery, bounce, unsubscribe, spam, open, click, reply stats under Marketing > Emails

**A/B test offers across the same list** (sales)
1. Add a random split (e.g. 50/50) in the workflow
2. Copy all actions into the second branch
3. Change the offer (e.g. free quote vs discount vs buy two get third free)
4. Check per-branch statistics to find the winner within about an hour
5. Reuse the winning offer for similar businesses in the same niche
> "you can know within an hour of sending this email which one crushes the best" — Chris Koerner (~11:54)

**Ongoing triggers beyond one-off blast** (operations)
1. Birthday emails with a 10% coupon
2. Customer anniversary messages
3. Stripe payment processed, delay 365 days, then reactivation sequence

**Find the client's list** (acquisition)
1. Suggest sources: QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, Square, POS email capture
2. Request a spreadsheet export of past customers

**Prove value with analytics screenshots** (sales)
1. Run the campaign in-house without mentioning GoHighLevel
2. Send the client screenshots of the stats
3. Use the success to upsell other services

## Lessons

- *offer_design*: Lead with a zero-risk, performance-based offer to remove the buyer's objections and get a foot in the door.
- *offer_design*: Spend effort on the client's offer to their customers, not just your pitch to the owner; the offer drives results.
- *validation_testing*: A/B testing is a differentiator small business owners rarely do; reuse winning offers across similar clients in the same niche.
- *scaling*: Use a free or cheap first campaign to land and expand into higher-value recurring services.
- *automation_ai*: Prebuilt templates let a non-expert deliver a seemingly complex service quickly, so the value is in sales and offers rather than tech.
- *niche_selection*: Choose niches where outcomes are visible (e.g. roofing) so performance fees are verifiable.
- *operations*: Keep sequences simple rather than complex.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Rev-share is hard to track because you cannot see the client's quotes or payments; use a flat fee floor.
- You cannot run this without a customer list.
- Offers proven for one niche may not transfer to regions with different seasonality.

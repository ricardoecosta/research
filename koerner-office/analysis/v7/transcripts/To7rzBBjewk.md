- **Startup cost:** not stated
- **Time to first revenue:** not stated

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Estimate directory ad revenue from traffic** (finance)
1. Get monthly visits from SimilarWeb
2. Multiply by pages per visit
3. Apply an RPM assumption (host uses $15)
4. Treat the result as a floor because sponsored listings usually earn more than display ads
> "I can punch in 1.1 million visitors a month, 4.3 page views per visit, and a $15 RPM" — Spencer Haws (~2:27)

**Go narrow geographically** (product)
1. Pick one state or region and one topic
2. Build the directory with map and listings
3. Add guides
4. Link sister directories for other regions or replicate the site per state

**Build a service business on top of a directory** (product)
1. Build and rank a niche directory
2. Use the SEO and lead-generation experience to launch an agency for others in the niche
3. Sell qualified leads or marketing to the same industry
> "when you build a directory, you might then be able to build a service on top of that" — Spencer Haws (~9:00)

**Funnel-gated results to capture emails** (acquisition)
1. Ask a series of qualifying questions (type of care, timing)
2. Increase commitment with each click
3. Require email registration before showing results

**Jump on a new technology wave with a directory** (acquisition)
1. Spot a freshly announced standard or technology
2. Quickly build a directory of related resources
3. Rank early while competition is low

## Lessons

- *finance_unit_economics*: Directories mainly earn from sponsored or higher-placement listings, with display ads as a baseline, so traffic-based ad estimates understate revenue.
- *niche_selection*: Very narrow niches can still produce small, steady income, but expect hundreds not thousands per month from display ads alone.
- *customer_acquisition*: A high share of direct and returning visitors signals a useful reference resource that builds repeat traffic.
- *niche_selection*: Personal experience in a niche gives a trust and content edge (Sober Nation).
- *scaling*: Directories can become lead-gen assets or springboards to adjacent services such as an agency.
- *validation_testing*: New technology waves create early directory opportunities; MCP.so reached ~52k visits in three months.
- *scaling*: Replicating a proven directory structure across regions or topics (sister state sites) is a low-risk scaling path.
- *customer_acquisition*: Collecting emails through a gated funnel builds a valuable audience asset.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Growjo revenue estimates may not be accurate; the $10M HappyCow figure is unverified.
- Large directories like Care.com are unrealistic to replicate, needing years and millions in investment.
- MCP.so's monetisation is unknown and it may be pre-revenue.

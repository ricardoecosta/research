### I1. Dumpster rental (roll-off)

- **Customer:** Roofers, remodelers, contractors, homeowners doing cleanouts
- **Problem:** Renovation and cleanout debris must be removed for a short time only
- **Offer:** 20-yard dumpster dropped before 7am, collected after about a week
- **Business model:** Per-rental weekly fee with overage charges for weight
- **Pricing:** Weekly rental with included tonnage (about 2 tons) plus per-ton overage. 20-yd dumpster range USD 275-700 per week; national median (USA Superior) USD 419 per week; median overage USD 76 per ton over Big haulers include about 7 days
- **Acquisition:** google_business_profile_maps: Google Business Profile with clear photos and clear price; partnerships: First calls typically come from contractors; Harry cold-called roofers from permit stack; cold_call: Called 10 roofers from permit records and booked three drops
- **Delivery:** Owner trucks the dumpster out with a roll-off trailer or hook truck, collects, empties at landfill and redeploys
- **Unit economics (claims):** ~$600/week top line per dumpster, owner keeps 30-50%; 5 dumpsters at $450, 60% utilisation = ~$5,800/month before costs (narrator arithmetic); Dumpsters cost $3,500-$6,500 each
- **Tools:** Google Business Profile, Roll-off trailer/hook truck
- **Startup cost:** Dumpsters $3,500-$6,500 each; roll-off trailer from ~$30,000 new; hook truck $120,000+
- **Time to first revenue:** First couple of months slow while permits, landfill account and insurance are sorted (Harry's first paid drop in month two)
- **UK applicability [E]:** 3/5 — UK equivalent is skip hire; requires waste carrier licence, permits for skips on public highway, and waste transfer notes; market is dominated by established skip firms.
- **Risks:** Weight overage can erase margin if contract silent; Permits, landfill account and insurance required; Casual competitors and big haulers; Truck and delivery are physical and time-bound
- **Automation [E]:** acquisition 3/5 (Google Business Profile, local SEO and ads are automatable; contractor relationships need some human touch.); onboarding 3/5 (Online booking, quote and e-sign contract with weight terms can be automated.); delivery 0/5 (Physically trucking, dropping and emptying bins requires a human driver every time.); billing 4/5 (Card on file, automatic overage invoicing from landfill tickets.); support 3/5 (Chatbot/phone agent can handle scheduling and pickup requests.)

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Weight limit clause in dumpster contract** (operations)
1. Include about 2 tons in the rental price
2. Charge a per-ton overage (national median about $76/ton)
3. Put weight limit in writing for every drop
> "A line in the contract with the included tonnage, usually about two tons, and a price for every ton over." — Harry (~10:34)

**Prospect contractors from building permits** (acquisition)
1. Review local permit records
2. Call roofers named on permits
3. Book drops for next month

**Prop rental catalogue and deposit** (operations)
1. Photograph and tag every piece on purchase day
2. Take a deposit before items leave
3. Discount long rentals (half price weeks 2-4)

**Turo mileage cap and protection plan choice** (pricing)
1. Set daily mileage limit with overage rate in listing
2. Start on safer protection plan, move up once trusting renters

**Quarterly rate reviews for storage** (pricing)
1. Review rates every quarter
2. Raise gently

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Assets that customers need only briefly create repeat revenue from the same owner and customers rarely want to compete.
- *scaling*: Start with the cheapest asset and let early rental income fund the next asset.
- *niche_selection*: Regulatory friction (permits, landfill accounts, zoning) acts as a moat against casual competitors.
- *offer_design*: Reliability and punctuality is the main brand against big incumbents in local rentals.
- *legal_risk*: Write contract terms (weight, mileage, deposits) to protect against asset wear and disputes.
- *validation_testing*: Validate your local market by counting competitors and checking their prices.
- *automation_ai*: Physical rental assets limit automation because delivery and cleaning need humans; storage is the most automatable.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Dumpster contract without weight limits can wipe out profit because landfills charge by the ton.
- Check city rules, permits and landfill requirements before first drop.
- Higher Turo host share means carrying more damage risk yourself.
- Setting storage prices once and forgetting them loses revenue.

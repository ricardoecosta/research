## Tactics and playbook steps

**Validate a personalised product across Etsy and Amazon** (product)
1. Use Everbee to find top-selling personalised products on Etsy
2. Check Amazon for consistent sales (e.g. '600 bought in past month')
3. Look for categories where existing designs look poor so better design wins
> "The next thing to do is to look at Amazon. Is Amazon selling it and does it sell consistently?" — George McConnell (~1:34)

**Three-step ChatGPT star map prompt** (operations)
1. Ask for a print-ready personalised star map poster with title, names, date, time and location
2. Step 1: determine the real prominent constellations visible from that location and time, only those above the horizon
3. Step 2: identify and label the visible constellations
4. Step 3: return final facts, poster text and a reusable image prompt; run the prompt to generate the art
> "determine the actual prominent constellation visible from the provided location on the provided day and time" — George McConnell (~3:01)

**Upscale before printing and fulfil via Gelato** (operations)
1. Upscale the image (he uses ARTD) so large prints are not grainy
2. In Gelato choose product (fine art poster, framed, 24x36), upload design, preview
3. Create mock-ups with ChatGPT
4. One-click connect Gelato to Etsy/Shopify/Amazon and enable the personalisation option for name, date, location
> "make sure that you're using a good upscaler no matter what. Upscale before you upload it" — George McConnell (~5:58)

## Lessons

- *offer_design*: Personalised products connect emotionally and turn a want into a need.
- *niche_selection*: Look for proven-demand products where incumbents' design quality is poor; better design is the wedge.
- *automation_ai*: Make the AI produce a reusable prompt template so each new order only needs new inputs.
- *customer_acquisition*: Launch gift products before Q4 to catch holiday demand.
- *validation_testing*: Cross-check demand on more than one marketplace before building.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Grainy, non-upscaled images on large prints will lose customers.
- Only include constellations really visible at that time and place; made-up content undermines the product.

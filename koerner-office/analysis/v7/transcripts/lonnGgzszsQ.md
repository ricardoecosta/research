## Tactics and playbook steps

**Choose a low-cost diversified fund** (product)
1. Consider an S&P 500 fund for exposure to large US companies, or a total-market fund for small and mid-size companies too.
2. Compare the expense ratio and keep it under 0.05% if possible, according to the narrator.
3. Choose a fund or ETF and turn on dividend reinvestment.
> "The simplest option is the S&P 500 index fund, which gives you exposure to the largest companies in the country." — Casper Capital narrator (~6:54)

**Match a fund to its brokerage** (finance)
1. Check whether the broker charges transaction fees to buy a fund.
2. The narrator recommends buying Vanguard funds through Vanguard and Schwab funds through Schwab to avoid stated transaction fees.
> "Always match your fund with the company's brokerage. So, Vanguard, buy it with Vanguard. Schwab's fund, buy it with Schwab so you pay $0 in fees." — Casper Capital narrator (~7:38)

**Set up recurring contributions** (operations)
1. Open a brokerage account and select the account type.
2. Search for the chosen ticker and decide how much to invest.
3. Turn on recurring investments and automatic monthly deposits so contributions continue consistently.
> "I would also recommend turning on automatic deposits every month. That's called dollar-cost averaging." — Casper Capital narrator (~9:02)

**Reinvest dividends and hold long term** (finance)
1. Enable dividend reinvestment so dividends buy more shares.
2. Avoid reacting to short-term market movements.
3. Sell only when money is needed for retirement or a major life event, according to the narrator.
> "The plan is to hold for years, ideally decades. Only sell if you truly need the money like during retirement or a major life event." — Casper Capital narrator (~9:47)

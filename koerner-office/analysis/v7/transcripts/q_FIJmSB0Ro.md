## Tactics and playbook steps

**Find legacy-system customers** (acquisition)
1. Use BuiltWith, Outscraper or chamber-of-commerce listings to find businesses on ancient systems
2. Contact the office manager, who hates the old system most
3. Pitch: off your old system in a weekend, zero downtime
4. Start with small local firms (about 13 employees) to get experience, then move up
> "use tools like built with or outscraper or a free chamber of commerce website to find businesses that are still running on ancient systems" — Chris Koerner (~13:14)

**Take bookkeepers' overflow cleanup work** (acquisition)
1. Join Facebook groups for bookkeepers
2. Offer to take cleanup work they turn down
3. Promise 48-hour turnaround
4. Run the AI reconciliation overnight and review before delivery
> "there are for bookkeepers that are just turning down what they call cleanup work because they hate it" — Chris Koerner (~15:03)

**Copy a proven human-run business with AI** (product)
1. Pick a business already making money with employees
2. Identify the labour-heavy step
3. Replace it with an AI model
4. Prototype now using current models like Opus 4.8

**Prepare during the blackout** (mindset)
1. Pick one niche, one offer and one landing page
2. Prototype with available models
3. Test several AI tools so you can compare them

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Look for businesses that already work with human labour, then substitute AI for the labour.
- *automation_ai*: Treat AI as the engine, not the company, and keep tools swappable.
- *scaling*: Start small with easy customers to gain experience before aiming at high-value clients.
- *scaling*: A productized service capped by one person's hours can scale if AI covers production.
- *mindset_execution*: Prepare with current tools instead of waiting for the better model.
- *niche_selection*: Tedious work that people avoid, such as bookkeeping cleanup, is a good opening for outsourced AI.
- *legal_risk*: Regulation or restriction of a technology can signal its importance, but it also brings supply and legal risk.

## Warnings and mistakes

- A model that was switched off once can be switched off or nerfed again, so don't depend on one AI tool.
- Don't start with large enterprise clients, and don't start with a customer that is your own employer.
- The model will make mistakes and need training before the work can be trusted.

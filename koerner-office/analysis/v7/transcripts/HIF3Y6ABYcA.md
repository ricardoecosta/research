## Tactics and playbook steps

**Multi-AI market validation** (product)
1. Write one specific research prompt naming review sources (Capterra, G2, Software Advice, TrustRadius) and asking for top 10 pain points, must-have features, opportunity map and viability
2. Run it in parallel in Perplexity Labs, Manus agent, Claude with deep research, and ChatGPT agent mode
3. Answer clarifying questions on target segment and differentiation
4. Compare outputs; apply the 80/20 rule to focus on the top two complaints
> "these AI tools are only as good as the prompts I give them, and they're only as good as the places I tell them to go and extract data from" — Chris Koerner (~1:37)

**Reddit complaint mining with GummySearch** (product)
1. Buy a $10 day pass
2. Create an audience of relevant subreddits (about 10M members)
3. Search the competitor name and use Patterns to summarise 100 submissions
4. Compare sentiment scores across competitors
5. Use Ask to find the most common complaint; turn its opposite into the marketing message
6. Track topics for email alerts
> "Summarize 100 submissions" — Chris Koerner (~7:59)

**Paid polling without an audience** (product)
1. Create a PickFu poll with a targeted question
2. Choose 100 respondents filtered to the target (e.g. US small business owners)
3. Pay about $1 per response; results within an hour
4. More targeting costs more

**8-minute waitlist landing page** (acquisition)
1. Duplicate an existing Carrd landing page
2. Add headline and email field
3. Connect a custom domain via Namecheap A and CNAME records
4. Connect Beehiiv via API key and publication ID
5. Collect UTM parameters to segment signups
6. Publish and test with a plus-addressed email
> "I'm going to show you how I personally launch landing pages that can start collecting emails in about 3 to four minutes" — Chris Koerner (~10:22)

**Search Facebook groups and Reddit manually** (product)
1. Search moderated small-business Facebook groups and niche subreddits for competitor names
2. Paste open post URLs into ChatGPT to summarise

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Validate demand cheaply before building: mine complaints, poll strangers and put up a waitlist.
- *offer_design*: Turn competitors' top complaint into your headline (e.g. 'cheap price forever' against rising prices).
- *niche_selection*: A commodity market is a massive market; you can win by executing basics properly rather than disrupting.
- *validation_testing*: Complaint lists follow 80/20: focus on the top two pain points.
- *niche_selection*: Serve a neglected segment (0-10 employee businesses) that incumbents serve poorly by trying to serve everyone.
- *automation_ai*: Specific prompts that name sources get better AI research output.
- *validation_testing*: Without an audience you can rent one through paid polling panels.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Accounting software is crowded and mature; customer acquisition will be expensive and bank integrations are complex.
- ChatGPT-5 won't use agentic features unless explicitly asked.
- Highly targeted PickFu polls cost more.
- Manus free plan is slow.

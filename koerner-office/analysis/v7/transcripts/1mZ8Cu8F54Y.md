### I1. Orbrush tongue cleaner via YouTube direct response

- **Time to first revenue:** not stated

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Beachhead on a small segment** (acquisition)
1. Identify a niche of a few percent of the market that wants the product
2. Treat that millions-strong segment as enough to clear inventory or launch
3. Expand from the beachhead
> "8% of people is like 30 million people just in the United States." — Jeff Harmon (~2:18)

**Skippable long-form ad as a filter** (acquisition)
1. Make entertaining content long enough to build investment
2. Run as skippable ad so only interested viewers watch
3. End with a call to action such as invest or buy

**Comedian-led ad retreat** (product)
1. Send product to 3-4 writers weeks ahead
2. Day 1 read scripts and pick the lead
3. Spend 3 days merging best elements
4. Cut any joke that does not advance the message
5. Lock all stakeholders in so approvals happen on-site

**Live social proof during fundraising** (sales)
1. Run a 24-48 hour live-stream marathon at the end of the raise
2. Pop up each new investment in real time with amount and location

**Sell the benefit not the product** (sales)
1. Frame the product as a tool to the desired outcome (kissing without bad-breath shame)

**Warm a celebrity face in the algorithm** (acquisition)
1. Have the celebrity repost older content to heat the face
2. Launch a fresh character account
3. Cut thousands of clips and take many swings

## Lessons

- *customer_acquisition*: Find a glitch: a channel or market where attention is underpriced and incumbents are not looking.
- *niche_selection*: Build products that solve your own problem (the user method); many top companies began that way.
- *niche_selection*: Small niches can build large businesses; two million paying members is enough.
- *validation_testing*: Test cheaply with real behaviour (skippable ad watch-through) to get signal before investing.
- *offer_design*: Earn attention first: let people skip, and you win those who stay.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Advertising spend of 8-12% is comparable to Wall Street raise fees; paid ads for fundraising is rational.
- *mindset_execution*: Letting go of disputes and keeping an abundance mentality frees focus for new opportunities.
- *automation_ai*: AI harnesses with guardrails let executives run agents unattended overnight; the bottleneck is human bandwidth.
- *hiring_outsourcing*: Cutting staff because of AI is a short-term play; productivity gains justify more hiring.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Early YouTube ad arbitrage no longer exists; the glitch constantly shifts.
- Early Orbrush Facebook page spamming and Google ads failed with ads costing $3-5 per $1 of sales.
- Lawsuits over unpaid invoices can drain 18 months to 3 years; mentor said unless over $5M it is not worth fighting.
- The movie business is a knife fight and Angel has been losing money while acquiring members.

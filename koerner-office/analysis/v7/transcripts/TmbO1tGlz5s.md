## Tactics and playbook steps

**Pre-list items before buying** (operations)
1. List the item on Facebook Marketplace before purchasing
2. Gauge demand from enquiries
3. Only buy the pallet once demand is confirmed
4. Use good photos/videos and optimised titles and descriptions
> "you can list it for sale before you even buy it to gauge exactly what the demand is" — Chris Koerner (~5:51)

**Use import records to find manufacturers** (acquisition)
1. Search the product in Import Yeti
2. Identify the manufacturing company shown
3. Offer sourcing as a service for a fee plus percentage of spend

**Fractional agency leverage** (operations)
1. Hire finance professionals
2. Place each on about five clients at $2.5k-$5.5k per month
3. Pay them about half the revenue

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Displaced workers can pivot into adjacent businesses where AI is a tool rather than a threat.
- *validation_testing*: Use Google Trends as a quick demand check for ideas like IV therapy and fractional services.
- *validation_testing*: Listing before buying inventory tests demand without capital risk.
- *pricing*: Recurring retainers (dashboards, fractional services, AI implementation) beat one-off fees for revenue stability.
- *automation_ai*: Selling AI implementation to SMBs works because owners know they need AI but lack skills; the tools are easy.
- *pricing*: A percentage-of-spend model aligns the agent with client outcomes, as with the 3% sourcing agent.
- *legal_risk*: Licensed healthcare ideas need a licensed person; non-licensed founders should look at tangential plays such as apps or coaching.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Clinical businesses (Botox, GLP-1) require licences such as NP, PA or MD, training, inventory and malpractice insurance.

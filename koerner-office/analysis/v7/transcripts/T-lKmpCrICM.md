## Tactics and playbook steps

**Build a consistent AI influencer with reference sheet** (product)
1. Generate a female AI influencer in Higgsfield (five free) choosing build, hair, ethnicity, age and features
2. Download and upload her to ChatGPT, asking it to keep everything the same and make three new outfits
3. Feed the outfit images back to ChatGPT to create a front/back/side/face reference sheet with realistic features
4. Reuse the sheet for all later image and video generation for consistency
> "you're going to ask ChatGPT to keep everything the same, but we're going to give her three new outfits" — George McConnell (~1:53)

**Pick proven TikTok Shop products and generate videos** (acquisition)
1. Browse TikTok Shop categories (e.g. women's leggings) and check units sold
2. Open a product and confirm creators earn commission
3. Add the product to your affiliate list
4. Ask ChatGPT to put the product on your AI influencer
5. Create video in Higgsfield using Seedance 2.5 with the reference sheet and a prompt describing the action
6. Optionally record yourself with the physical product and map it to the character with the Higgsfield tool, then change the voice
> "you take this item right here, and you just add it to your affiliate." — George McConnell (~4:33)

## Lessons

- *validation_testing*: Choose products by proven sales volume and visible creator commissions rather than guessing demand.
- *automation_ai*: A reference sheet (front, back, side, face) keeps an AI character consistent across many outfits and videos.
- *customer_acquisition*: Affiliate income scales with audience size, so audience-building is the real bottleneck, not content production.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Free tool tiers lower startup cost, but the creator's promoted link may be an affiliate link, so evaluate it critically.
- *legal_risk*: AI influencers for UK use must comply with advertising disclosure rules; undisclosed AI personas reviewing products they have not used are a legal and platform risk.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Video-to-character transfer keeps the original speaker's voice, so a male voice would play on a female character; you must change the voice.
- TikTok affiliates need 5,000 followers before starting, so there is a delay before any income.

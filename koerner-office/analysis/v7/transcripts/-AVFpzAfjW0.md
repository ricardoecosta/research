### I1. Freelance Salesforce consulting for mission-driven organisations (managed services)

## Tactics and playbook steps

**LinkedIn informational-interview outreach** (acquisition)
1. Target peers and hiring managers at organisations in your former industry
2. Engage with their posts first
3. Personalise: mention their career path
4. Say why you're reaching out
5. Always end with an ask for a 15-30 minute conversation
> "I always leave with, hey, would you be open to a 15 minute conversation or a 30 minute conversation?" — Hermela (~49:25)

**Scope a minimum monthly retainer** (pricing)
1. During sales, scope recurring work together
2. Set a fixed monthly hours minimum they really need
3. Bill extra hours as add-ons
4. Use enhancement projects on top

**Niche by former industry** (acquisition)
1. Leverage 10+ years in nonprofits/higher ed
2. Pitch 'I was your client'
3. Target consultancies serving that industry
> "I already know the clients you're going after because I was that client" — Hermela (~14:55)

**Negotiate reduced hours** (mindset)
1. Accept offer enthusiastically
2. Ask to reduce to 24 hours with pro-rated pay
3. Use freed days to build freelance clients

## Lessons

- *finance_unit_economics*: Mind the gap: keep expenses low and raise income to invest the difference.
- *niche_selection*: Pivot to adjacent skills, translating existing experience (industry knowledge, soft skills).
- *customer_acquisition*: Networking beats applying; offers came before certification.
- *mindset_execution*: Treat your day job as your largest client; use freed time to win more.
- *offer_design*: Recurring retainers weather-proof a consulting business.
- *automation_ai*: Business analysis (translating problems into requirements) gains value with AI.
- *mindset_execution*: Learn from someone who has done it (courses) to shortcut setup.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Coast FI: front-load investing so you can work less later.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Salary progression in institutions can be capped by budgets and waiting for retirements.
- Project-only work is feast or famine; prefer recurring managed services.
- The Salesforce market is more crowded and starting salaries lower than a few years ago.
- Blind job applications get filtered out; none of her offers came from them.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Bring a real bid first, then sell the relationship** (sales)
1. Text a list of partner businesses (e.g. roofers, builders) asking if they have projects needing your trade
2. Call every positive reply immediately to qualify the bid
3. Text prospective client businesses saying you have a live bid and want to partner
4. Call replies at once and pitch partner introductions as relationships, not leads
> "Bring them a project and then they believe in the value." — Chris Koerner (~14:11)

**Cold call setup in GoHighLevel** (operations)
1. Use the built-in power dialer
2. Set ring time to 15 seconds
3. Do not leave voicemails; retry later
4. Add custom dispositions (picked up, pitched, angry) to track

**Upgrade from per-appointment to guaranteed package** (pricing)
1. Start low per appointment to learn close rates
2. Package 10 intros at $6,000
3. Guarantee two real bids in the first 20 appointments
4. Replace partners that fail to deliver a quote in time
5. Add $250-500/month nurturing
6. Offer a menu close (20/40/120 appointments)

**Commission-only caller** (hiring)
1. Pay a caller roughly 30% of each appointment fee
2. No base salary

## Lessons

- *offer_design*: Prove value before asking for money: bringing a live bid first earns trust and attention.
- *pricing*: Start with a low price to learn close rates, then raise sharply once you see client demand for volume.
- *pricing*: Aim for 10x-1000x ROI for the customer, not 2-3x.
- *customer_acquisition*: Cold outreach is only as hard as your offer.
- *offer_design*: Sell the relationship, not the lead; this differentiates from shared-lead marketplaces.
- *scaling*: Add recurring revenue (nurturing) to a one-off service so success doesn't put you out of business.
- *niche_selection*: Pick a niche you are excited about rather than the shiniest object on the platform.
- *niche_selection*: Work with an existing client, observe the value chain and spot the upstream/downstream opportunity.

## Warnings and mistakes

- Gatekeepers and project managers are not incentivised, so follow-up calls to them close poorly; target owners.
- Prospects often think it is a scam or lead gen company; frame as relationship partnership.
- Cold text laws (TCPA in the US) are looser for B2B but still a risk; UK PECR is stricter for sole traders.

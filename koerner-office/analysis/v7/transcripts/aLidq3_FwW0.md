## Tactics and playbook steps

**Find a familiar problem in a fragmented category** (validation_testing)
1. Start with a category you care about and already use.
2. Ask whether customers are using several separate apps for one job.
3. Build a unified product if the gap is meaningful, even if competitors already exist.
> "There were a few different apps that people were using. There wasn't really one unified platform, so I just went out and built it." — Max (~5:10)

**Charge for the app instead of relying on forgotten cancellations** (pricing)
1. Set an upfront yearly fee.
2. Explain the annual charge as a simple monthly equivalent.
3. Max says this avoids depending on users forgetting to cancel.
> "So we just charged an upfront $24.99 a yearly fee, which is just really $2 a month." — Max (~1:52)

**Use founder-made videos to test creative** (acquisition)
1. Film several short videos every day rather than paying creators before the product and message are clear.
2. Track hold rate and skip rate for each video.
3. Use the strongest formats as the basis for further promotion.
> "I was looking at all of the videos I posted and analyzed what's the hold rate? What's the skip rate? And then I started running the best performers as paid ads." — Max (~7:03)

**Scale ad spend after signs of product-market fit** (finance_unit_economics)
1. Start paid promotion with a low budget.
2. Watch for evidence that the product and creative are working.
3. Increase spend once performance supports it; Max reported spending around USD 250 a day at the time of the interview.
> "I started on a low budget at first. But as I started to see that there really was product market fit. I started to still be up to budget and now we're spending around $250 a day on paid ads." — Max (~7:11)

**Delay hiring UGC creators until the message is clear** (hiring_outsourcing)
1. First define the app's scope and learn which features drive downloads.
2. Identify the angles and formats that work.
3. Only then consider paying UGC creators, to avoid burning money on unproven creative.
> "I would say we don't really have the scope defined of your app yet. And you don't understand what features are going to drive downloads. It's very early on, I would avoid hiring UGC creators." — Max (~7:44)

**Prioritise Meta over TikTok based on the app’s results** (acquisition)
1. Compare paid results across platforms instead of assuming they perform alike.
2. For this app, Max preferred Meta because he believed its views were more valuable and its buyers had higher intent and willingness to pay.
> "I would say right now, met as much better than TikTok. I think the views are more valuable. I think they're higher intent buyers." — Max (~8:01)

**Share the founder journey publicly** (acquisition)
1. Post progress and lessons on X consistently.
2. Share what is working, what is not, and vulnerabilities rather than hiding the business.
3. Use founder-led content to help prospective customers follow the product's development.
> "Be as public about it as possible. Show everyone what's working, what's not. Tell them about your vulnerabilities. People are really supportive." — Max (~9:05)

**Use clipping to widen organic reach** (acquisition)
1. Turn existing video material into clips for broader distribution.
2. Max says clipping can bring international exposure and organic app downloads.
> "This is a great way to get international exposure and a great way to get organic downloads." — Max (~8:19)

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: A crowded market can still have room for a product that makes a fragmented user experience feel unified.
- *customer_acquisition*: A founder’s personal brand and visible journey can help attract users and build support around a product.
- *validation_testing*: Use your own content to discover which messages work before hiring creators to repeat them.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Start paid marketing at a small scale and increase spending when there is evidence that the product and creative are working.
- *niche_selection*: Do not assume that an idea needs to be completely new; passion and a useful execution can matter in an established category.
- *mindset_execution*: Share what is working and what is not; Max says people can be supportive rather than simply copying the idea.
- *automation_ai*: AI coding tools can lower the barrier to building a first app for someone without much coding experience.
- *mindset_execution*: A founder who is reluctant to market may delay learning; Max recommends making promotion part of the work from the start.
- *pricing*: Charging upfront can avoid relying on customers forgetting to cancel, though the interview does not give retention or renewal data.
- *customer_acquisition*: Compare platforms using the business’s own audience and purchase results; Max preferred Meta to TikTok for this app.

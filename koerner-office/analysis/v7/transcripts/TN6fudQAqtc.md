## Tactics and playbook steps

**Choose a product with a structural domestic advantage** (niche_selection)
1. Look at the physical characteristics of a product and its shipping costs relative to its price.
2. Compare the product's logistics needs with the geography of the market.
3. Choose a product where domestic production and local presence can be an advantage.
> "Air filters have a very specific characteristic. They're bulky and they're cheap relative to what it cost to ship them." — David Heacock (~7:36)

**Build an HVAC business from a trade skill** (operations)
1. Train and work as an HVAC technician at an established company.
2. Move into 1099 work, run a crew, and take installation jobs.
3. Build a brand, customer base, and book of business.
4. If growth is desired, expand into neighboring cities or buy businesses in the same trade from owners who want to exit.
> "Step two is you go 1099. You run your own crew and you start taking installation jobs." — David Heacock (~21:29)

**Choose a paid skill before taking on education debt** (finance)
1. Ask what work the degree or training enables you to do.
2. Check whether someone will pay for that work.
3. If you cannot identify paid work at the end, do not sign the loan.
> "At the end of this, what can I do that somebody will pay me for? If you can't answer it, don't sign the loan." — David Heacock (~14:11)

**Set a life goal before selecting a business** (offer_design)
1. Decide what you want your life to look like.
2. Choose a business or career that can support that life.
3. Keep learning and adjust as the economy and customer value change.
> "Decide what you actually want your life to look like before you pick the business, not after." — David Heacock (~27:25)

## Lessons

- *offer_design*: Choose work where you can explain the value you create and who benefits from it.
- *niche_selection*: Look for structural advantages in the product and its logistics rather than choosing a business only because of personal passion.
- *other*: A trade can provide a paid skill and a path into ownership without requiring a degree or business debt.
- *finance_unit_economics*: Debt can reduce the freedom to retrain, take a pay cut, move, or pursue a new opportunity.
- *mindset_execution*: Build skills continuously and respond to what people value now, whether you work for yourself or someone else.
- *mindset_execution*: Do not wait for promotion or someone else to provide a complete playbook; take specific action and responsibility for your direction.
- *offer_design*: Design a business around the life you want, rather than letting the business dictate your life.
- *scaling*: Reliable, patient execution in a practical trade can create a route from employment to ownership and eventual sale.

## Tactics and playbook steps

**Research a proven niche before building** (validation_testing)
1. Follow experienced app founders on X and study the tactics they share.
2. Use Sensor Tower to research possible app niches and competing apps.
3. Look for a niche where many apps consistently earn over $10,000 per month.
> "And after a ton of apps and that niche consistently doing over 10K a month, then that's the niche you want to build in." — Lino (~8:56)

**Validate demand with posts before building** (validation_testing)
1. Create a faceless TikTok account for the idea.
2. Post three to four niche slideshows per day for a week.
3. Before the product exists, replace the download call to action with a request to comment for the app.
4. Treat repeated comments as a demand signal before investing in the build.
> "I would make a faceless TikTok page and start promoting your app through organic TikTok slideshows and start posting three to four times a day." — Lino (~9:10)

**Ship a first version quickly** (product)
1. Build with a tool you can use and aim to ship within seven days.
2. Launch before adding extra features such as a leaderboard or sound app.
3. After launch, improve the product while tracking metrics and marketing.
> "I would try to ship in seven days or less." — Lino (~10:06)

**Test creator content cheaply** (acquisition)
1. Find small Instagram and TikTok creators in the niche who post consistently.
2. Try creators with 500 to 1,000 followers.
3. Pay about $100 for 10 videos and test different hooks, formats, and calls to action.
4. Identify a winning content style before putting serious money behind it.
> "I would reach out to them and pay them like $100 for 10 videos and have them test out a bunch of different hooks, formats and CTAs for my app." — Lino (~10:44)

**Use a creator partner for distribution** (acquisition)
1. Consider partnering with one established creator who has relevant distribution.
2. Offer equity so the creator has an ongoing stake in the business and content.

**Scale a creator roster and paid ads** (acquisition)
1. After finding a content style that works, source creators directly or use a UGC platform.
2. Lino recommends a VA to contact creators who fit the criteria: 5 to 50,000 followers, consistent posting, showing their face, and an active comment section.
3. Build a roster of around 10 to 15 consistent creators.
4. Run paid ads behind the resulting creative.
> "Now, once you're sitting at around 10 to 15 creators posting consistently and you have a bunch of creatives, now you can start running paid ads behind those creatives." — Lino (~11:31)

**Shift budget toward the ads that perform** (acquisition)
1. Cut the influencer deals that are not working.
2. Keep the creator whose content works and put paid distribution behind the content.
3. Test the same creative on Meta and TikTok, then shift spend toward the stronger platform for the target audience.

**Improve the product after marketing stabilizes** (product)
1. Do not spend months polishing before launch.

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Choose a narrow customer problem rather than building a broad tracker; the founder attributes strong performance to the app’s niche focus.
- *validation_testing*: Check whether a niche already has several successful apps before committing to build in it.
- *validation_testing*: Test interest with content and a clear call to action before spending time building the product.
- *customer_acquisition*: Find a repeatable creator-content format before spending heavily on UGC or paid distribution.
- *customer_acquisition*: Creator partnerships can give a founder distribution while aligning the creator through an equity stake.
- *customer_acquisition*: Compare performance on different ad platforms using the same creatives and audience fit, then scale the channel that works better.
- *mindset_execution*: Launch a simple version quickly, then improve it using real usage and marketing data.
- *mindset_execution*: Do not compare your early progress with other founders’ later-stage results; focus on the next step and keep improving.
- *mindset_execution*: Keep showing up and getting better through difficult periods rather than treating a setback as proof the business cannot work.

## Lessons

- *niche_selection*: Look for services customers already search for that local businesses offer but do not target with dedicated pages.
- *customer_acquisition*: A focused service website can support a business built around one local job type.
- *scaling*: A local service company can expand its coverage by routing jobs to trusted subcontractors in different areas.
- *customer_acquisition*: Product sellers can be useful referral partners when their customers need a separate installation or moving service.
- *customer_acquisition*: Customer photos and recommendations in local communities can help a visually demonstrable service spread.
- *legal_risk*: Keep dangerous physical work with professionals rather than treating it as a simple do-it-yourself service.

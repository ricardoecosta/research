### 1. One Shopify add-on app (Start now)
**Why.** It has the best fit and the best success score in the whole set. Shopify takes 0% revenue share on the first $1M, merchants find apps through the store, and selling to businesses keeps it mostly outside the DMCC consumer-subscription rules. The validation's central case is £500–£2k in year one, with a 35–45% chance of £0.

**First test (£0).**
- Agents scan about 40 UK or B2B Shopify search terms and cluster the 1–2★ reviews.
- **Pass:** at least one term meets all of these:
  - the incumbent is ≤3.8★ with ≥30 reviews, or hasn't been updated in 12+ months;
  - there are ≤8 relevant apps, and ≤1 launched in the last 6 months;
  - a paid tier exists at ≥$7.99/month;
  - one complaint theme appears in ≥10 reviews;
  - the MVP takes ≤2 weekends.
- **Fail:** no term passes. Rerun once on WordPress.org, then drop the idea.
- **Later gates:** by day 30, the app is approved with ≥15 installs and ≥1 paying merchant. By day 90, ≥5 paying merchants.
- **New input from the gems:** look for the neglected segment that experiences the problem as a painkiller, Voicy's move.

### 2. One single-purpose iOS app (Start now, capped)
**Why.** This is validated, and it reuses the same gap-mining agents as #1. The vault gem adds a proven, agent-run acquisition loop: each morning an agent finds Search Console queries with impressions but no clicks, then writes at most one quality guide a day. The validation's most likely outcome is under £1,500 for the year. Platform risks: guideline 4.3 spam, review delays, and the DMCC rules from 1 Jan 2027.

**First test.** Agents mine about 100 keywords, including about 20 vault and privacy terms. The Apple fee is about £79/year.
- **Pass:** ≥5 keywords with popularity ≥30, ≤2 top-10 apps with the keyword in the title, and ≥1 top-10 app estimated at ≥$1k/month.
- **Fail:** fewer than 3 qualifying keywords. Stop.
- **Then:** one hard-paywall app. Stop after two 4.2 or 4.3 rejections.

### 3. Encrypted vault app (Next, inside #2)
**Why.** Vaultaire's revenue looks credible, and it is agent-run: SEO, on-device delivery, lifetime pricing. But the founder is an ex-Google serial founder, Keepsafe and Private Photo Vault are entrenched, and new vibe-coded rivals already run the same SEO play. UK encryption law is unsettled while Apple's IPT challenge is pending, with a hearing in Dec 2026.

**First test.** Use #2's gate restricted to vault keywords.
- **Pass:** ≥3 vault keywords meet #2's thresholds, and ≥3 of the top-10 apps haven't been updated in 12+ months.
- **Fail:** drop vault as a niche.

### 4. UK data-led directory with calculators as feeder pages (Next)
**Why.** It is legally clean and highly automatable, but depends entirely on Google. The validation estimates £0–£2k in 12 months, most likely low hundreds. Calculators work only as feeder pages on the same domain.

**First test (~£10 for a domain).** Agents check 3 niches with an OGL dataset, 30 long-tail queries each.
- **Pass:** at least one niche meets all of these:
  - the licence is confirmed in writing;
  - ≥10 of 30 queries have no joined answer in the top 5 and no AI Overview that fully answers them;
  - Keyword Planner demand is ≥10k searches/month across the set;
  - no outbound sales are needed.
- **Fail:** shelve the idea.

### 5. Find a Tender expiring-contracts alerts for one trade (Next)
**Why.** It has a high fit, open government data and no cold outreach. The broad version failed. The narrow version depends on whether contract end dates are actually usable, which is still unknown. The validation estimates £0–£3k, with £0 the most likely single outcome.

**First test (£0).** Agents pull 24 months of award notices for one CPV group.
- **Pass:** ≥60% have usable end dates and ≥50 contracts expire in the next 12 months.
- **Fail:** drop the idea.
- **If it passes, a 14-day page test:**
  - **Pass:** ≥40 signups, ≥2 paid pre-orders and ≥5 "useful" replies.
  - **Fail:** <15 signups or no payments.

### 6. Narrow AI implementation service (Park)
**Why.** It is the most-cited idea in the set, at 14 videos, and the fastest route to a first paying client. DocsBot's founder says the money is in implementation, at about $2k setup and $500/month. But the UK is crowded: 276 agencies in one directory, with a median £1,500 starting project and £297/month plan. It needs human sales and delivery, which breaks the minimal-involvement brief.

**First test, if opted in.**

- **Pass:** ≥1 paid setup at ≥£750 within 21 days.
- **Fail:** none.

### 7. Inbound call and lead-response agent (Park)

The likely buyers are sole traders, who can't be cold-emailed under Ricardo's rule. And it's local-business work, which Ricardo finds boring. It only makes sense folded into #6 for a higher-value limited-company vertical.

**First test.** Use the relaxed list's test only if #6 is opted into and a vertical with high job values is chosen (`TOP10_relaxed.md` §6).

### 8. Templates and skill packs (Park)

**First test.** None standalone.

## Recommendation

**Start with #1, the Shopify add-on, as the main bet. Add #2, the single iOS app with vault keywords in its gate, as a capped second experiment from week 2.** Both use the same gap-mining agents and inbound marketplace demand, and neither needs cold outreach or Ricardo on camera.

If both fail their gates by week 4, move to #5 and then #4. Only switch to #6 if Ricardo decides he'll spend about 5 hours a week on sales.

**Spend cap for the four weeks:** about £150. That covers the Shopify Partner fee ($19, paid later), the Apple fee (about £79), a domain (about £10) and tools (£0–£50). The figures come from the validation files, and the Apple fee comes from a third-party UK guide.

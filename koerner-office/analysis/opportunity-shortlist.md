## Best fits for your constraints

### 1. AI-built conversion websites for local trades — best adjacent playbook

**What:** Use an AI agent to find local service businesses with strong Google reviews but weak/no websites; sell a one-page conversion site with booking and tap-to-call, then a monthly retainer.

**Why it fits**
- Low startup cost.
- Demo-first: build a sample site before outreach.
- Async-friendly: email/LinkedIn/video.
- Reuses the same prospect research, offer, and approval workflow as ClarityRelay.
- Fast cash: setup fee + monthly.

**Risks**
- Crowded web-design market.
- Low reply rates to trades.
- Free demos can be copied.
- Clients may expect phone calls.

**Source videos:** `DlNWYzaL_F0`, `GZbjmWNmIkc`

### 2. Niche micro-SaaS — highest scalability

**What:** Build a tiny app for one painful workflow (e.g. digital liability waivers for youth groups), sold as a subscription or per-event fee.

**Why it fits**
- Extremely low startup cost.
- Async delivery.
- Scales without linear labour.
- Can be built with AI coding tools.

**Risks**
- Slow/uncertain first revenue.
- Existing competitors (DocuSign, Smartwaiver).
- Maintenance and GDPR/child-data issues.
- Requires distribution, not just building.

**Source videos:** `UItZIyRpWSw`, `rvlCcsqmlM4`, `AXatzxlPc8Y`, `MpbxMuH3VHk`

### 3. White-label GoHighLevel for one niche — strongest recurring model

**What:** Rebrand GoHighLevel as a niche CRM/booking/AI phone system; sell $200–$500/month subscriptions.

**Why it fits**
- Recurring revenue.
- High margins after platform cost.
- One niche can be productized.
- Async sales possible.

**Risks**
- Platform dependency.
- Support load and churn.
- Crowded.
- Revenue evidence is based on public award tiers/estimates, not audited.

**Source video:** `VOo3M3ZHUoA`

### 4. Email/newsletter automation — low-effort add-on

**What:** Use AI + Beehiiv to turn a small business's dormant email list into campaigns, automations, and a community; charge a monthly retainer.

**Why it fits**
- Low startup cost.
- Async delivery.
- Can be sold to existing clients as an upsell.

**Risks**
- Weak evidence: revenue example is hypothetical.
- Proving ROI.
- Sales may need calls.

**Source video:** `zEmmeIWMNvQ`

### 5. Remote-managed cleaning — cash-flow, not low-effort

**What:** Run a cleaning brand remotely; contractors clean, you handle sales/scheduling/support.

**Why it fits**
- Recurring revenue.
- Strong cash flow.
- Proven case studies.
- Can be run from a distance.

**Risks**
- Contractor reliability/quality.
- UK employment status and insurance.
- Local ops and phone calls.
- Promotional source (paid coaching academy).

**Source videos:** `S7T8GlIEkFs`, `Nc-1LMAwYYo`, `TR5bJ083igY`, `eu2TagmN9fU`, `ha_jQR9idBA`, `9U4MCbfL8MQ`, `8XSaVl6QnVA`, `p6mYFoY7fQE`, `ejCWJqJ8nKk`

## Recommended next playbooks to ask for

If you want a second playbook beyond ClarityRelay, ask for these in order:

1. **AI conversion websites for local trades** — fastest adjacent play, reuses your current assets.
2. **Niche micro-SaaS** — highest scalability; validate demand first.
3. **White-label GoHighLevel for one niche** — best recurring revenue, but support-heavy.
4. **Email/newsletter automation** — best low-effort upsell, weakest evidence.

Do **not** start with cleaning, home services, trailer leasing, 3D printing, or event rentals unless you want an operations business rather than an async software/service business.

## What to ask the playbook for

For the chosen opportunity, request:
- ICP and buyer
- offer and pricing
- niche-selection scorecard
- first 30 prospects
- outreach scripts
- demo/validation workflow
- delivery SOP
- QA/acceptance gate
- 21-day launch plan
- metrics and kill criteria

The existing ClarityRelay manual already covers the AI support-agent version; the next playbook should reuse its structure.

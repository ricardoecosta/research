## Why chalkboard ads was missed earlier

It was **not in the first 60-video selection**. The first pass ranked videos by title keywords and only mined the top 60. Chalkboard/community boards came from `McQucrnkSt0` — *The Simplest $10K+/M Business You’ve Never Heard Of* — which scored low on the title filter.

It is now included as **“Restaurant community boards / chalkboard ad boards”** and ranks **#93 of 227** with a weighted score of **3.45**.

Why it sits mid-table:

- **Good:** ~$3,000–$3,600/year per board; 200+ boards placed; $0/minimal startup cost; ~$10K/month case within six months.
- **Bad for you:** local in-person selling to restaurants and advertisers; low async fit; UK ad prices likely lower; renewals depend on advertiser results.
- It is a real, proven offline business — just not the best fit for a solo, async, 5–10-hour/week operator.

## Best for your constraints

### Build-first websites for local businesses on a monthly subscription — 4.20

Use AI to build a website for a local business before contacting them, show it to the owner, and charge a monthly fee to keep it live.

- Customer: Local SMBs with no website or a poor one (trades, services)
- Money: Recurring $50–$100/month per site (example price $75/month), with near-zero marginal cost per client
- Main risk: Cold texting is restricted under UK/EU rules (PECR/GDPR); outreach should use email or LinkedIn with care

### API arbitrage / Apify actor wrapper — 4.15

Wrap one pre-built scraper/AI actor in a clean niche website and charge a subscription or credits.

- Customer: SDRs, agencies, recruiters, e-commerce brands, local-service lead buyers
- Money: $35/month or credit packs; actor/API cost is pennies per run; ~95% gross margin
- Main risk: Easy to copy

### Niche Google Maps lead scraper — 4.15

A scraper for one local vertical (dentists, roofers, gyms, lawyers) that exports a clean lead list.

- Customer: SDRs, agencies, local-service businesses, recruiters
- Money: $35/month or credit packs that do not expire
- Main risk: Crowded

### Zapier/OpenAI customer-service bot for Shopify — 4.05

A simple automation that replies to customer-service emails using the store FAQ, policies, and past replies.

- Customer: Shopify stores and small e-commerce brands
- Money: $100/month per store; Zapier/OpenAI costs are low
- Main risk: Email hallucination

### Physical gifting as B2B outreach (cake delivery) — 4.00

An automated service that sends cakes from local bakeries to prospects or clients so B2B sellers stand out from AI-generated outreach.

- Customer: B2B sales teams and companies doing outreach or client gifting
- Money: Per-delivery fees and corporate bulk orders, with a margin over the bakery's price
- Main risk: Food logistics and quality control

### AI customer-support agent setup + monthly retainer for local SMBs — 3.95

Set up AI chat/support agents on an existing white-label platform for local businesses, then charge a monthly fee to keep them running.

- Customer: Local small businesses that handle repetitive customer enquiries
- Money: About $2,000–3,000 one-off setup fee plus $500–1,000/month retainer
- Main risk: The guest sells the platform, so the claims are self-interested

### White-label GoHighLevel CRM for one local trade — 3.95

Rebrand HighLevel's all-in-one marketing, booking and CRM software as a product for one local trade and sell it as a monthly subscription.

- Customer: Local service businesses such as med spas, pest control, dentists, roofers, bounce house rental, dog walkers
- Money: Monthly subscription. The example is $39/month. The public awards page shows gold-tier resellers have 100+ paying customers (at least $3,900/month), and some resellers make $50–150K/month profit by the presenter's back-of-napkin estimate
- Main risk: Crowded market with many identical resellers

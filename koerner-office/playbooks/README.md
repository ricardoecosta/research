# Playbooks

Operator playbooks for the worthy ideas found in mined transcripts, plus the three ideas picked for fully automated validation. Each one is grounded only in its source transcripts. Creator figures are marked as claims, and UK notes are marked as not from the video.

**Template:** the outline in `../analysis/opportunity-shortlist.md` ("What to ask the playbook for"): ICP and buyer, offer and pricing, niche-selection scorecard, first 30 prospects, outreach scripts, demo/validation workflow, delivery SOP, QA/acceptance gate, 21-day launch plan, metrics and kill criteria. The original ClarityRelay manual was not available, so its exact format could not be copied. The automation playbooks add **§11 Automation stack and automated proving-phase workflow**.

**Worthy rule used:** a new idea in the overall top 30, or one with strong real revenue evidence and a low-cost start, that does not duplicate an idea already ranked or already covered by a playbook.
- The missed-call agent was included with a caveat: it has only price claims and no closed sale, and it overlaps ClarityRelay.
- In the 30-video run, the only new top-30 idea (AI voice-agent receptionist, #25) duplicates the missed-call playbook, so it was added there as a note.
- Photo magnets were the strongest new revenue evidence with an under-$1,000 start.
- Other ideas with revenue claims were not given playbooks because they need heavy capex or physical crews and score low on founder fit: forestry mulching, deck building, parking-lot striping, the jerky route and junk removal.

**Automation picks:** the three best-ranked ideas whose probe, outreach and delivery of the first sales can run on software and agents with little manual work. See §11 in each, and the reasoning in `../analysis/all_ideas_ranked.md` ("Best ideas to prove with full automation").

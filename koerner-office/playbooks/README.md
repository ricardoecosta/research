# Playbooks

Operator playbooks for the worthy ideas found in newly mined transcripts. Each one is grounded only in its source transcript. Creator figures are marked as claims, and UK notes are marked as not from the video.

**Template:** the outline in `../analysis/opportunity-shortlist.md` ("What to ask the playbook for"): ICP and buyer, offer and pricing, niche-selection scorecard, first 30 prospects, outreach scripts, demo/validation workflow, delivery SOP, QA/acceptance gate, 21-day launch plan, metrics and kill criteria. The original ClarityRelay manual was not available, so its exact format could not be copied.

| `ai-missed-call-text-back.md` | AI missed-call text-back agent for local trades | #22 / 3.90 | `g4-3AxelI_Y` |

**Worthy rule used:** a new idea in the overall top 30 (or scoring ≥ 3.9), with revenue evidence, that does not duplicate an idea already ranked. The missed-call agent is included with a caveat: it has only price claims and no closed sale, and it overlaps ClarityRelay.

# Plan: explore build-first websites for local businesses (Bromley, side-hustle pace)

21 Oct 2026. Idea #1 of 227 (4.20) in `../analysis/all_ideas_ranked.md`. Playbook: `../playbooks/build-first-websites-local-business.md`.
**[E]** = my estimate or assumption, not from a source. Creator figures are claims, cited by video ID (transcripts in `../transcripts/raw/`).

## Bottom line (honest)

- **Best evidence:** `1PwTrINNzOA`. An agent found 350 Utah businesses, built 350 sites and mailed QR postcards. 341 arrived, 68 scanned ("like 20%"), and **a part-time salesperson's phone calls** closed about 14–17. That gave "a little over 8K a month" in MRR. Those customers bought a **marketing package** ("three to 700 bucks a month", a few at "1,200") with the site thrown in free. They did not buy a £50 website.
- **Weaker evidence:** `_aGMxMmJJj4` gives the $75/month website pricing, but the presenter made **no sale on camera**.
- **The UK market is already crowded at the low end.** "Free build, pay monthly" sites sell from **£24.99–£29/month** with £0 setup (aposlostudios.com, digitaltwo.co.uk, checked Oct 2026). Typical managed plans are £25–£150/month. So a bare website isn't new. What's different here is that the owner **sees their own finished site first, made by someone local**.
- **What to prove in 6 weeks:** at ~6 h/week, can Bromley owners be made to look at a preview, and will some of them pay £49/month for it? Website-only income is capped [E]: 20 customers × £49 ≈ £1k MRR. The bigger money is in upsells, which both videos say too. Treat the website as the foot in the door.
- **Cost of finding out:** ≈ **£175–£240 cash** (capped at £300) and **≈ 38 hours** over 6 weeks. At the £30/h you used in `mrr-plan-v2.md`, that time is worth ≈ £1.1k.

## 1. Hypotheses and kill/continue criteria

| # | Hypothesis | Pass mark [E] | Evidence anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Owners will open a preview of their own site | ≥15% of contacted prospects view it | ~20% postcard scans (`1PwTrINNzOA`) |
| H2 | Viewers will pay £49/month (or the one-off) | ≥10% of viewers pay within 21 days | ~20% of scanners closed, but by phone and on a bigger package (`1PwTrINNzOA`) |
| H3 | Builds are cheap enough to do at volume | ≤10 min of human review per preview by week 3; ≤£0.50 cash per preview, excluding post | "a handful of cents" per site in tokens; "10 20 bucks" for 350 (`1PwTrINNzOA`) |
| H4 | Delivery and care fit around a toddler | ≤1 h to launch a new customer; ≤20 min per customer per month | [E] |

**Gates:**
- **Day 7 (after the probes in §2):** stop if all three probes are dead: 0 of 10 walk-ins want the link or a price, under 5% of postcards are scanned, and no email clicks.
- **Week 4:** continue if there are **≥2 paying customers** (or ≥£100 MRR). If views are ≥15% but nobody has paid, rethink and run one variant for 2 weeks: add a screened call step, or offer "free build, £29/month", or lead with Growth at £99. **Kill if views are under 8% after 100 previews.**
- **Week 6:** scale to monthly waves only if there are **≥3–5 paying customers**, the cost to win a customer is **≤£150** (≈ 3 months of £49), and H3 and H4 hold. Otherwise stop and write up what you learned.

## 2. The 3 cheapest probes in the first 7 days

| Probe | What | Cash | Measure | Signal to look for [E] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1: 10 doors** (Sat, day 6) | 10 previews for independent high-street service businesses in Beckenham, Bromley High St and Orpington that have no site or a weak one (from the scrape: website empty or social-only, ≥20 reviews). Walk in at a quiet time and show the site on your phone. | ~£20 leave-behind cards | wanted the link · asked the price · paid or booked a follow-up | ≥3 ask the price; ≥1 pays or agrees to start |
| **P2: 40 QR postcards** (posted day 3) | Stannp A6 postcard to 40 trades **with a postal address** (sole traders and Ltd), each with a QR code to that business's preview | 40 × £0.90 = **£36** | scans (QR has `?s=card`) · claim clicks · calls in | ≥15% scan by day 12 |
| **P3: 30 emails to Ltd companies** (day 4) | Companies House confirms each one is a live **Ltd/LLP** (a PECR corporate subscriber), and its email is published on the site or listing. Plain-text email with the preview link. | £0 | unique link clicks · replies · checkouts | ≥25% click, ≥2 replies |

Anyone who scans or clicks gets **one live call**, but only after their number has been screened against TPS and CTPS (see §5). That's the step that closed the deals in `1PwTrINNzOA` (about 90% of closes came from scanners).
**Why these three:** walking in is the close the presenter recommends ("deliver it in person. It makes the close so much easier", `_aGMxMmJJj4`). The postcard copies the only funnel with real numbers. Email to Ltd companies is the free, legal, automatable channel.

## 4. Finding prospects (automated)

1. **Scrape:** the Apify `compass/crawler-google-places` actor. It costs $4 per 1,000 places on the Free plan, and the $5 monthly free credit covers ≈ 1,250 places (Apify pricing, Sep 2026). Query "[trade]" × Bromley, Beckenham, Orpington, Chislehurst, West Wickham, Petts Wood, Penge, Hayes and Biggin Hill. Keep name, phone, website, address or service-area flag, category, rating, review count and `place_id`. The Maps playbook (`../playbooks/apify-actor-wrapper-maps-lead-scraper.md`) covers the same pipeline, so this script can later be reused as that product.
2. **Qualify (script):** drop chains and franchises, and anything under 10 reviews. Tag each website as one of:
   - **none**
   - **social or directory only** (Facebook, Checkatrade, Yell, MyBuilder)
   - **broken** (no HTTPS, errors, PageSpeed mobile < 50 [E threshold], copyright year ≤ 2019)
   - **fine** (dropped)
3. **Company check:** the Companies House public API (free key) gives company type and status. **Ltd/LLP → email allowed.** Sole trader or partnership (no match) → post, walk-in or a screened call only.
4. **Choosing the trade [E]:** Bromley borough is suburban and full of houses, so jobs are high-ticket: **landscaping, fencing, driveways and paving, roofing, heating/boiler engineers, electricians**. One extra job pays for a year of £49/month, which makes the pitch easy. Pick the one trade with the most "none + broken" listings that have ≥20 reviews. The presenter picked handymen because owners list their own mobiles and have a budget (`_aGMxMmJJj4`). For walk-ins, use whichever high-street services the scrape shows without sites (e.g. tailors, florists, dog groomers, beauty, cafés).
5. **Split:** half weak site and half no site. The sources disagree here. `_aGMxMmJJj4` says to target owners who already have a site ("they realize the value of a website"). `1PwTrINNzOA` did both and lost the data on which converted. Tag every preview so this time you know.

## 5. Outreach rules (UK) and scripts

**Rules** (ICO B2B guidance, checked 21 Oct 2026; re-read before sending):
- **Email:** OK to Ltd/LLP addresses without consent, as long as you identify yourself and include an opt-out. **Not OK** to sole traders or ordinary partnerships without consent.
- **Texts and WhatsApp:** the same rules apply, so **no cold texts** at all. The presenter says mass texting "is not compliant. You will get sued" (`1PwTrINNzOA`).
- **Live calls:** OK to business numbers only after screening them against **TPS and CTPS** (sole traders register on TPS) and against your own do-not-call list. Say who you are. No automated or ringless calls.
- **Post and walk-ins:** outside PECR. UK GDPR still applies: use legitimate interest, keep a short privacy notice on the site, and keep a suppression list.
- **Registration:** the ICO data protection fee (Tier 1, £52/year, £47 by direct debit) is very likely needed.

**Walk-in (P1):**
> "Hi, I'm Ricardo, I live in Bromley. I make websites for local businesses, and I made one for you already. Can I show you? [phone] It's live on a test address. If you like it, it's £49 a month: hosting, your domain and small changes included, and you can cancel any time. If not, no worries, I'll delete it."

**Postcard (P2):**
- **Front:** "[Business name], we built you a new website. Scan to see it." [QR code]
- **Back:**
  > "Made in Bromley by Ricardo Costa. It's yours for £49/month: domain, hosting and edits included, nothing upfront. Not interested? Ignore this and it's deleted in 30 days. [phone] · [email] · [privacy URL]"

**Email (P3, Ltd only):**
> Subject: I built [Business name] a new website
>
> "Hi [first name], I live in [Bromley/Beckenham] and built this for [Business name] last week: [link]. It's on a test address. If you'd like it on your domain, it's £49/month with hosting and edits, and you only pay once you've approved it. If not, reply 'no' and I'll take it down.
> Ricardo Costa · [address] · Reply 'stop' and I won't email again."
>
> **Follow-up (day 7):** "Did you get a chance to look? [link]. It comes down on [date]."

**Call (scanners and clickers only, after screening):**
> "Hi, is that [name]? It's Ricardo, I live in Bromley. You looked at the website I made for [business]. Was it close to what you'd want? … What would you change? … I can put it live on your own domain this week for £49 a month."

Opening with "I live 20 minutes from you" is the line the presenter calls "the most critical sentence" (`_aGMxMmJJj4`).

## 6. Build stack and the fast template workflow

| Job | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Site template | One static HTML/Tailwind (or Astro) template per trade: hero with tap-to-call, services, areas covered, a rating summary linking to their Google reviews, FAQ, contact | £0 |
| Copy | A cheap LLM fills a JSON per prospect from the listing data (services, areas, FAQ). Rules: **never invent** years, awards, prices or accreditations (Gas Safe, NICEIC, TrustMark), and don't copy their review text or photos. Use stock photos until onboarding. | ~£5–20 per 150 [E] |
| Hosting | Cloudflare Pages, one preview subdomain per prospect on a cheap brand domain, `noindex`, deleted after 30 days | £0 + ~£10 domain [E] |
| Claim and pay | A banner on every preview: "Built for [X] by Ricardo Costa, Bromley. Claim it for £49/month" → a Stripe Payment Link with the prospect ID | Card fee 1.5% + 20p (Stripe UK); plus Stripe Billing on subscriptions [check rate] |
| Tracking | Cloudflare Web Analytics, a unique URL per prospect, `?s=card/email/door`, and a funnel CSV or Google Sheet | £0 |
| Post | Stannp API (`api-eu1.stannp.com`, `test=true` first) | £0.90 per A6 postcard |

**Per-batch workflow** (the box agent can run it; you approve batches):
1. scrape
2. qualify and Companies House check
3. generate JSON
4. render and deploy
5. **auto-QA:** name, phone and town match the listing; no banned claims; links work; mobile Lighthouse ≥ 90 [E]
6. **you review** 10 random previews and the send list (~15 min)
7. send
8. daily funnel summary

The presenter builds one master prompt and reuses it: "Swap out the name of the business and the location and you are good to go" (`_aGMxMmJJj4`). GoHighLevel, which sponsored that video, is optional. Defer it until upsells justify a CRM.

- **Onboarding:**

  2. Send a 5-question form: domain, logo and photos, services, areas, hours.

## 8. What to measure

For each channel (door, card, email) and each segment (none, social-only, broken), track:
- funnel: prospects pulled → qualified → previews → contacted → delivered or seen → **viewed** → claim clicks → replies or calls → **paid** → churned
- time per preview and per customer
- cash cost per preview and per paying customer
- MRR
- reasons given for "no"
- upsell yes-rate

Keep it in `plans/funnel.csv` so it can be compared with the 20% view / ~20% close benchmark from `1PwTrINNzOA`.

## 10. Risks and mitigations

- **Crowded at £25–29/month nationally.** Compete on "already built, local, seen first", not on price. If price is the objection, test the £29 "free build" variant once (week-4 rethink).
- **The proven close was a human phone call** (`1PwTrINNzOA`), and calls are the scarce hours with a toddler. Call only warm prospects (scanners and clickers), in two lunch slots a week.
- **Many trades hide their address** (service-area listings), so they can't get a postcard. Email them if they're Ltd; otherwise make a screened call or skip.
- **Previews can look like a scam or impersonation.** Label every preview, set `noindex`, delete it after 30 days, include your real name and town, and never copy their photos or reviews.
- **Invented claims on a preview** (for example a fake "Gas Safe registered") are a legal and reputational problem. The QA gate blocks them.
- **Churn, and support creep** (edits, email, "can you do my Facebook"). Stick to 2 edits a month and sell anything else as Growth.
- **Burnout or time slip.** The gates are fixed dates. If two weeks in a row slip, that counts as a "kill" signal for a side hustle.
